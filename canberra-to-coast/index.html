What's in every fence we build

A Colorbond fence is only as good as the bits you can't see. Tap a part.

  • Steel posts set in concrete, deep. Canberra frost heaves shallow footings, so we go deeper than the bare minimum. A post that moves is a fence that leans.
  • Top and bottom rails, plus a middle rail on taller panels, hold the sheets rigid so they don't drum and rattle in the wind.
  • Genuine Colorbond steel, cut to your height, in the colour you picked. Not the cheap import sheet that fades in two summers.
  • A treated timber or steel plinth along the ground keeps soil, mulch and mower damage off the steel, so the fence doesn't rust from the bottom up.
  • Capping covers the sharp top edge and finishes the line clean. Post caps keep water out of the posts.

Good to know

The questions Damian gets asked at nearly every quote.

How much does a Colorbond fence cost?
Most 1.8 m Colorbond fences in Canberra land between $95 and $135 a metre installed, so a typical 30 m side boundary is roughly $2,900 to $4,000. Taller fences, sloping blocks and rock cost a bit more. Try the price guide for your numbers, then Damian gives you a fixed price on site.
Do I have to pay for the whole boundary fence myself?
Usually not. In the ACT, neighbours normally share the cost of a boundary fence equally, and it's similar in NSW for Queanbeyan. Talk to your neighbour before the quote. Damian writes the quote so you can hand a copy straight over.
Do I need council approval?
Most side and back boundary fences in the ACT don't need approval as long as they sit behind the front of the house and stay under the height limit. Front fences and corner blocks have extra rules. If yours needs a tick from anyone, Damian will tell you at the quote.
How long does it take?
Damian usually quotes within a few days of you getting in touch. Most home fences are built in one to two days once materials are in. Colorbond sheets are cut to your height, so the colour and height you choose set the lead time.
Colorbond or timber?
Colorbond doesn't rot, warp or feed termites, never needs painting, and looks the same from both sides. Timber is cheaper up front and dearer over ten years. If you want the timber look, ask about a timber plinth under Colorbond sheets.
My block slopes. Does that matter?
Not much. We either step the panels down the slope or rake them to follow it, and fill gaps with a plinth. Have a look at the sloping jobs in the photos above.
Do you take the old fence away?
Yes. Pulling down the old fence and taking it to the tip is part of every replacement quote, tip fees included.
Is there a warranty?
Genuine Colorbond steel comes with BlueScope's own warranty against rust and fading. Damian stands behind his workmanship too, and he's easy to get hold of if anything ever needs a look.
Can I get a gate to match?
Yes. Single gates for the side of the house, double gates wide enough for a car or trailer, all in the same colour as the fence and hung so they still swing straight in five years.
