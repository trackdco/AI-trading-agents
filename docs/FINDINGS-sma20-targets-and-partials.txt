TARGET SIZE AND TRADE MANAGEMENT — 20-SMA PIN BAR
Config: 1-min, SIDE trend, W1, buf 2 ticks, min stop 8 pts, cost 0.75, max hold 120 min.
Exits scanned from the bar AFTER entry; a bar touching both stop and target counts STOP.

================ WHY THE WIN RATE LOOKED LOW ================
It looked low because every result up to now used a 3R target, where break-even is 25%.
The win rate at 1R is exactly what a discretionary trader would expect.

Order flow + small signal bar filter, 2023-26 footprint days (n as shown):
  target   n    win    break-even   edge (pts)   R/trade
    1R    90   52.2%     50.0%         +2.2       -0.017
  1.5R    90   47.8%     40.0%         +7.8       +0.133
    2R    90   41.1%     33.3%         +7.8       +0.172
  2.5R    90   35.6%     28.6%         +7.0       +0.183
    3R    90   32.2%     25.0%         +7.2       +0.227

At 1R you need 53.1% to cover 0.75 pt on a 12 pt stop. We get 52.2%. Short by a point.

THE STRUCTURAL POINT
The filters add +7 to +8 points of edge at 1.5R and beyond, but only +2.2 at 1R. So the
edge is not "price bounces here" - it is "price CONTINUES after it bounces". A 1R target
exits before the information pays.

Corollary, and it is counterintuitive: ORDER FLOW MAKES 1R WORSE.
  raw at 1R              51.3% win
  order-flow filtered    49.0% win
The order-flow filter selects for trades that travel FAR, not trades that go RIGHT.
Pairing it with a 1R target is actively counterproductive.

================ TRADE MANAGEMENT SCHEMES (all three tapes) ================
                        2017-19   2020-22   2023-26   share finishing positive
  flat 1R               +0.0321   +0.0047   -0.0212        52-55%
  flat 3R               -0.0258   -0.0676   +0.0711        25-28%
  half@1R -> 3R         +0.0032   -0.0315   +0.0250        25-28%
  half@1R, BE, -> 3R    +0.0611   +0.0047   +0.0189        51-55%   <- only all-positive
  half@1R -> 5R         -0.0397   +0.0186   +0.0211        17-20%
  third@1R -> 3R        -0.0065   -0.0435   +0.0403        25-28%

Share of pins that reach 1R at all: 55.1% / 52.9% / 51.5% - stable across nine years.

WHY THE BREAKEVEN MOVE IS LOAD-BEARING
Without it, banking half at 1R and then getting stopped gives back the +0.5R, so the
trade nets negative - which is why "half@1R -> 3R" has the same 26% positive rate as a
flat 3R. Move the stop to entry and a failed runner costs nothing, so the booked half
survives. That single change is what converts a 26% scheme into a 52% scheme AND makes
it the only one positive on all three tapes.

================ WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH ================
- Magnitude is small: +0.061 / +0.005 / +0.019, averaging +0.028 R. 2020-22 is ~zero.
- The breakeven stop is flattered by bar data. If a 1-min bar spikes to 1R and returns
  through entry within the same bar, this code books the partial and only applies the
  BE stop from the NEXT bar. Real sequencing could stop at breakeven first. Sizing that
  error needs 1-second data on a sample.
- Six schemes were compared. One clearing on all three tapes is roughly what chance
  would give, so this is a lead, not a result.
- The partial test ran WITHOUT order flow, since footprint only covers 2023-26.
