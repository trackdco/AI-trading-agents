Read this honestly

  • The pass rate answers variance, not edge. These paths resample our own 82 winning 2026 trades — so it proves the sizing survives normal variance, not that the edge repeats live. That's the only real risk and no MC can test it.
  • This sizing is ~1.67× the 6/3/3 scheme (same 2:1 tier ratio). Faster to target, bigger swings: a wide-stop full E-mini loss can be $~$1,200 — up to ~60% of the buffer in one trade. Watch that the DD stays comfortable, not just the pass rate.
  • Not modelled: intraday unrealized drawdown (small here — one position at a time, ~$100–350 open risk), firm consistency rules (e.g. Apex 30%/day), and edge decay.
  • Actual 2026 sequence: PASS — finished $+13,273 over 82 trades.
