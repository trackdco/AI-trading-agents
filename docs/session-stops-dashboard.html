Does the CVD gate help on top? No.

Once the stops are right, CVD is redundant — it removes winners.

Stops and CVD are substitutes, not complements. The CVD gate was a proxy for "avoid bad trades"; proper stops do that better. Every CVD variant lowers P&L (it cut 33 trades worth +$5,228 for a 2-point win-rate bump) and some break a green month. The low win rate is the cost of the big runners — it's profitable, and filtering it kills winners too.
