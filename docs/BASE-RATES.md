**Withdrawal (2026-08-07):** the LTF-recensus declaration listed BR-19..BR-26,
sweep_b (BR-15) and the locus ranking (BR-11/12) as *invalidated pending
re-measurement at the true trigger resolution*. **That invalidation is
withdrawn.** The re-census measured the trigger timeframe directly and the 15m
candle won monotonically, so those base rates were measured on the right
population after all. BR-27..BR-31 are what replaced the concern.

**Standing principle (2026-08-07):** three raw mechanisms measured (failed
