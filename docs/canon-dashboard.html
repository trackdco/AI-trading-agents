The playbook — what a good vs bad setup looks like

Pre-market 04:00–09:30

universal limit-retest entry · A / B / B2 · stop 7–60pt · median stop ~14pt
A-grade (score 4–5 → 1.0–1.5 lots)
  • No wall behind the entry — the book is empty where you came from (flagship check)
  • Fill-bar delta agrees with the trade
  • Last-15-min tape not running against you
  • Entry on the right side of VWAP geometry
  • Pre-market window CVD confirms direction
No-trade
  • Score ≤2 — two or fewer checks: 12% WR, pure bleed
  • Stop <7pt (toxic noise) or >60pt (0/12 ever reached 2R)
  • Trade #2+ of the day without score ≥4

Golden window 09:45–10:30

same entry & patterns · median stop ~24–30pt · needs structure AND quality
A-grade (gscore ≥3 and Q ≥3 → step up, cap 1.5)
  • A big wall (≥7 lots) sits AHEAD of the trade — a magnet to trade into. Opposite of pre!
  • Volatility expanding at entry, overnight extreme old, clean 30-min approach, 15-min tape agrees
  • Quality stack: big impulse minute at fill, fill aligned or absorption fill into counter-flow, busy trigger tape (>11/30m), VWAP extension, London delta-slope agrees
No-trade
  • gscore ≤2 — the reject pile ran −$31k/2yr at 0–17% WR
  • Q ≤1 — structure without quality: 12/16 losers incl. every 53–56pt monster
  • Only wall on the book is behind you: 14%/12% WR, −$282 avg both years

Shared skeleton: trade #2+ needs score ≥4 · day P&L <0 → both structure checks required (A-setups exempt) · −$400 day stop · 3-min in-trade cut · cold-streak governor ×0.5 · good-PA boost ×1.5.
