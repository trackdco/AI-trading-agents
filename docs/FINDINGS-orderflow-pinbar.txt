================ THE ACTUAL FINDING ================
The strongest thing order flow gives us is a rule for what NOT to take, not a rule for
what to take. The 15% of pin bars with neither absorption in the wick nor net selling
across the bar lose 0.38 R each at t = -3.15. Removing them lifts a book from -0.02 to
+0.13 without touching anything else.

Pat's mechanism is REAL: 85-89% of these pin bars do show the aggressive side hitting
into the wick and failing. But because it is present on nearly every pin, it cannot
rank them - it can only flag the minority where it is missing, and those are the ones
that bleed.

The counterintuitive half is B. The best pins are the ones where the aggressive side
was in control for the WHOLE bar and still could not close it their way. If net flow
has already flipped to our side by the close, the move has happened and we are late.

================ WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH ================
- Standalone t is +1.43, not > 2.5. This is real but not proven.
- Zero coverage before 2023-07. No out-of-sample test in time is possible with the
  footprint data we hold.
- The 3-min surfaces are thin (148-252 trades) and 3m_SLOPE showed the weakest gap.
