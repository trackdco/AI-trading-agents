What this sim assumes

  • The sample is the backtest, not the future. Every trade (stop size + result) is resampled from the 921-session certified run (§32 spec + §35 arming, guard rails G3/G5/G6, 0.5pt round-trip cost per contract). The haircut slider is the built-in dose of doubt.
  • Drawdown is checked end-of-day only, trailing the EOD high-water mark, and in both phases the floor locks at the start balance once you're up the drawdown amount — the Lucid frame.
  • The funded phase restarts a fresh balance under the same rule; “payout” means the funded target was reached after the minimum funded days, banking min(withdraw% × profit, max payout).
  • Sizing is quantized to whole MNQ micros ($2/pt). Flat mode passes stop-size variance straight into $ risk (stops run 7.2pt median, 30pt max by construction); budget mode floors to affordable micros and skips what doesn't fit. Switch modes to see the cost of quantization against the idealized $/R stream.
  • The embedded stream already carries the in-engine 30pt cap and 1R arming (§34–35: the limit rests dark until price runs 1R past the level — 19% fewer trades, +30% expectancy, maxDD −18.1R → −14.0R). The stop-cap field can only tighten further from 30; here a tighter cap drops trades post-hoc, so live would be the same or slightly better.
  • Days are trading days (~21 per month), every day trades the full three-book NQ program (the GC book is not in this stream), no missed sessions, no discretion.
  • This era is the optimistic drawdown regime. The 2020–22 replication (§36) earned the same per-trade expectancy but drew down 2.2× deeper (−30R armed, with losses chaining across days in late 2022 — something 2023–26 never did). Whatever size this page suggests, the robust number is about half of it until sizing is re-derived across both eras.
