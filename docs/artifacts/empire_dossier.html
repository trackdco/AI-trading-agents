01 The frozen spec doc §19 · §34 · §35

Grammar — 1-minute candle closes ≥ depth through a reference level → limit rests at the retest, armed only after price has run 1R (the trade's own risk) beyond the level → structural stop one tick past the close-through candle's extreme (prior-candle escalation; 5pt floor, 30pt cap — wider-stop signals are never placed) → 1R full exit → stop-and-reverse on an opposing crossing close → high-impact news gate 08:00–09:30 ET → pendings die 16:00, flat by session end.

Books — three: an 8-level book (prior-day VA / POC / high-low + weekly VA / POC, proximity-merged), session-anchored VWAP bands (±1σ, ±2σ), and a 09:30-anchored NY VWAP. Guard rails: first-in-wins dedupe within one floor, global cap 4, same-direction cap 3.

Honesty stack — fills count only when price trades a full tick through the level; 0.5pt/RT cost; same-bar ambiguity scored as a loss; stop checked before target inside every bar; risk-normalized sizing.

05 Walk-forward §37 · §39

With selection (252d rolling IS → 63d OOS, pick best grid cell by IS Sharpe, 20 folds): WFE = 1.01 — the out-of-sample edge is 101% of in-sample. 20/20 folds positive. The roll re-picks the exact frozen cell in 18 of 20 folds, and both deviations underperformed it out-of-sample — re-optimizing is worse than freezing. Frozen persistence (no selection): OOS/IS 1.133, 23/23 folds, 27/27 calendar quarters. Edge decay: none — frozen-cell OOS R/day regressed on time slopes positive (+0.028/fold, t = +1.76). Across seven years there is no decay to extrapolate.

09 Standing caveats — read before believing any of it §36 · §38 · queue findings

  • Sim ≠ live. Tick-through fills and a 0.5pt overlay are the model's floor, but there is no queue or latency model. MBP-10 depth (Apr 2026: median 3 contracts resting at a level; these levels only ~10% thicker than anywhere else) supports the 1-tick fill assumption at retail size — and the edge dies at 2–4 ticks of required penetration. The paper bridge's first job is logging fill penetration per trade.
  • Executor latency is load-bearing: the arming check must fire and the order reach the exchange within the same minute the arming bar closes; acting one bar late costs a third of the edge.
  • The drawdown envelope is regime-dependent. Union-tape literal path: 3 micros fit a $2,000 trailing account; bootstrapped ≥80%-odds sizing on the calm era alone says more. Size to the union, not to 2023-26.
  • The rail pass is post-hoc chronological; the exact joint sim of the three books is executor-stage work.
  • ~60 trades/day is automation-only. News gate is inert pre-2023 (archive starts 2023-01). No untouched forward holdout exists for arming — 2017–2019 is preregistered as its first true out-of-sample era.
