================ IT DOES NOT SHARE THE FILL-BAR BUG ================
This was the first thing checked, because the book predates the 2026-09-04 fill-bar finding
by five weeks. It is CLEAN on that specific failure, and the evidence is unambiguous:

  304 of 1,874 fit trades (16.2%) have exit_ts == fill_ts. Their composition:
      247 stops, 57 targets. Win rate among them 18.8% (vs 49.6% for the rest).
      Mean R +0.149 vs +0.400 for the rest. They DRAG the book down.
  Removing every zero-hold trade:            meanR +0.359 -> +0.400, PF 1.860 -> 1.941
  Removing all same-bar resolution entirely: meanR +0.359 -> +0.567, PF 1.860 -> 2.571

The empire's bug ran the other way: 74% of its TARGET wins were same-bar and removing them
destroyed the result. Here same-bar handling is CONSERVATIVE - the book is better without
it. A same-bar stop on a limit fill (price trades down to the limit and continues to the
stop) is a real loss and is correctly booked as one.

The genuinely lookahead-prone subset is small: 57 same-bar TARGET exits, 3.0% of the book.
Those should be re-scored with exits scanned from the bar after the fill.

================ VERDICT ================
RETEST ONCE - and it is the best candidate in the repo, ahead of both previously reported
survivors. Two things must happen before any number here is quoted:
  1. Re-score the 57 same-bar TARGET exits with --exit-next-bar semantics. Expected impact

  2. Explain the Sharpe. 5.9-7.6 annualised with a 13/13 green-month record is the profile
