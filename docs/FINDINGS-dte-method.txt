FINDING 1 — DELIVERY IS THE WHOLE EDGE, AND ONLY ON THE HIGHER TIMEFRAMES.
D on the 1h or 4h FVG is positive on all three tapes (+0.058/+0.117/+0.158 and
+0.085/+0.108/+0.162). It flips a losing trigger into a winning one. The 15m version
FAILS 2017-19 (-0.113) and "any of the three" inherits that failure. Higher timeframe is
strictly better: 4h > 1h >> 15m.

FINDING 2 — LOW-RESISTANCE LIQUIDITY DOES NOTHING. IT SLIGHTLY HURTS.
The source calls it "the most important aspect of this whole video" and "if I do not have
lower resistance liquidity, I do not take a trade". Measured, it is NEGATIVE on all three
tapes and every threshold:
  LRLQ >= 1  -0.061   >= 2  -0.061   >= 3  -0.054   >= 4  -0.037  (2023-26)
Prediction P2 said it would move R by less than 0.05. It moved it by 0.001.

FULL DTE pooled across all three tapes: n=2,676, R +0.0619, win 35.7% (break-even 33.3%,
so +2.4 points of excess), PF 1.095, t +2.23, maxDD 64.8R, drop-3-best +0.0597.
It fails the pre-registered bar: NOT positive on all three tapes (2017-19 is -0.030),

================ ORDER FLOW: NOTHING, AND FOR A STRUCTURAL REASON ================
Footprint attached to the 2023-26 trades (1,766 with coverage):
  wick absorption delta   gap +0.020   corr with R -0.014
  delta over the V window gap +0.008   corr -0.011
  delta over inversion bar gap +0.150  corr +0.027   but n=37 versus n=1,729

Only 2% of DTE signals have delta running against the trade. On the 20-SMA pin bar it was
39%. The reason is structural: DTE REQUIRES a displacement candle closing in the trade
direction, and that condition selects for delta agreeing. The pin-bar edge came from the
opposite population — a candle closing one way while aggression ran the other. DTE's own
trigger definition excludes the thing that made order flow useful. Order flow cannot help
this strategy, and that is a property of the rule, not of the data.

================ THE AUTOPSY, AND A CORRECTION ================
23 features screened on 2023-26, then re-checked per tape. What SURVIVES a same-sign test
across all three tapes:
  V window length (vwin)   +0.032 / +0.027 / +0.123   LONGER V is better
  ATR at entry             +0.158 / +0.059 / +0.122   higher volatility is better
Winners have a median 3-candle V, losers 2. The source stresses a fast "quick up, quick
down" move; faster is measurably worse.

WHAT DOES NOT SURVIVE — CORRECTING A CLAIM MADE EARLIER THIS SESSION.
On 2023-26 alone, distance from the 20 SMA (t +2.37), fib 0.5 (t +2.20), fib 0.705
(t +2.01) and fib 0.382 (t +1.97) all said the same thing: being FAR from a level is
better than being near it. I reported that as a finding and linked it to the prior-day POC
result on the pin bar ("that's now twice"). It DOES NOT REPLICATE:
  d_sma20     -0.051 / -0.078 / +0.104   sign flips
  d_fib0.5    -0.026 / +0.005 / +0.154   sign flips
  d_fib0.618  +0.086 / -0.007 / +0.089   sign flips
  d_vwap      -0.038 / -0.081 / +0.070   sign flips
That was a single-tape artifact and the claim is withdrawn. 23 features at |t|>1.95 give
about 1 false positive by chance; I found 4 and at least 3 of them were noise.

VWAP bands added nothing anywhere: five measurements (VWAP, +/-1sd, +/-2sd, sd position),
best t 1.61 on the discovery tape, no replication.

================ VERDICT ================
The strategy as taught is not what works in it. The execution trigger loses on all three
tapes. The gate the source calls most important is worthless. The gate that does the work
is higher-timeframe fair-value-gap delivery on the 1h and 4h, which is positive on all
three tapes but only reaches t 2.23 pooled and fails 2017-19 in its full form.

Not dead, and the one surviving component is worth isolating: D on 1h/4h alone, without
the LRLQ gate, scores +0.058/+0.117/+0.158. That is the next test, plus the random control.
