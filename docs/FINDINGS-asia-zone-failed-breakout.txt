WHAT THE DIAGNOSTIC SAYS
The reversion does not happen. Raw forward moves after the signal are +1 to +5 points on 2023-26
(t = 1.0 to 1.8, inside noise), NEGATIVE on 2020-22 (-2.6 at 15 min, t = -2.4), and flat-to-negative
on 2017-19. There is no consistent drift back toward the envelope in any horizon on any tape. So the
failure is not the stop placement or the target choice - the move the strategy is built to catch is
not in the data.

The 64-70% "hit the near edge" number is the trap. Price does touch the near edge most of the time,
which is what makes the setup look right on a chart. But the near edge is only ~0.22R away, so those
wins are tiny, and the 30-36% that miss it lose a full R. 0.66 x 0.23 = 0.15, nowhere near 1.0.
The same arithmetic holds at the VWAP line (0.52) and the far edge (0.63): every target is a losing
bet, just at different odds. This is the third time the win-rate x reward product has landed under 1
in this repository, from three unrelated entry rules.

STRUCTURAL NOTE, same as the previous test: the stop at the excursion extreme is 7.2 / 7.5 / 2.5
points median. Because the signal is a close BACK INSIDE the band, the extreme is by construction
very close to the entry. Any "stop beyond the recent extreme" rule built on a reversal candle will
produce a noise-width stop. That is a property of the rule shape, not of this particular idea.
