IS THE ORDER-FLOW FILTER REAL? — the follow-up the last verdict demanded (2026-09-08)

Context: docs/FINDINGS-random-control-verdict.txt showed the pin-bar ENTRY carries no
information — it loses to a matched random entry on 4 of 5 surfaces. The only thing that
ever moved its number was the order-flow filter. So the question became: is that filter a
real edge, or is it just selecting bars with a particular delta sign, which would work on
ANY entry and collapse the whole family?

Test: apply the SAME filter to matched RANDOM entries. Bar delta is defined on any bar, so
this is a fair comparison. (The wick-absorption half needs a wick and has no random
counterpart, so only the bar-delta half is tested — on both arms.)
Matched: same day, same NY window, same DIRECTION, same risk in points, same 3R target,
same max hold, same 0.75pt cost. Only the entry bar is randomised. 10 randoms per signal.

================ RESULT: THE FILTER AND THE PATTERN INTERACT ================
2023-26 footprint days, 1-min, W1, min stop 8pt.

  trend gate   filter lift on RANDOM   filter lift on PIN BAR   edge (both filtered)
  SIDE               -0.0322                  +0.1424                 +0.1306
  SLOPE              +0.0304                  +0.1715                 +0.1650
  NONE               +0.0282                  +0.1484                 +0.1288

The filter is worth roughly ZERO on random entries (-0.03 to +0.03) and +0.14 to +0.17 on
pin bars. Five-fold difference, same direction on all three gates.

CAVEAT ON THOSE THREE ROWS: the gates are NESTED (NONE contains SLOPE contains SIDE), so
they are one surface seen three ways, not three confirmations. The real check is the time
split below.

================ TIME SPLIT (NONE surface, 1,019 signals, cut 2025-10-27) ================
                        pin bar        random        EDGE
  FIRST HALF  filtered   +0.0884       -0.0256      +0.1140   (n=188 vs 2,574)
              unfiltered -0.0729       -0.0432      -0.0297   (n=509 vs 5,090)
  SECOND HALF filtered   +0.1663       +0.0255      +0.1408   (n=199 vs 2,507)
              unfiltered +0.0329       -0.0140      +0.0469   (n=510 vs 5,100)

Both halves say the same thing: unfiltered, the pin bar is worth nothing against random
(-0.030 and +0.047). Filtered, it is worth +0.114 and +0.141.

================ WHAT IS ACTUALLY TRUE ================
  the pin bar alone            worthless   (loses to random on 4 of 5 surfaces)
  bar delta alone              worthless   (lifts random entries by ~0)
  pin bar AND bar delta        +0.13 to +0.17 R over filtered random

This is an INTERACTION, not an additive edge. Neither ingredient works by itself. The
reading: a hammer that closes back through the SMA while net aggression over the whole bar
was still AGAINST the trade is a different event from either a hammer or a negative-delta
bar. The candle says where; the delta says the move was fought and the fight was lost by
the side that had control.

Declared condition 6 of docs/PREREG-sma20-pinbar-reclaim.md ("beats a matched random entry
by > 0.10 R") FAILS for the pin bar alone and PASSES for the filtered pin bar on every
surface and in both time halves.

================ WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH ================
- t-stats on the filtered real arms are 1.24 / 1.89 / 1.38. Individually not significant.
  The random arms are tight (n 1,621-5,081); the real arms are the noisy side.
- Footprint coverage begins 2023-07. There is NO out-of-sample test in time available. The
  2017-19 and 2020-22 tapes cannot be used because no order-flow data exists for them.
- Only the bar-delta half was tested. The wick-absorption half of the filter remains
  untested against any null and cannot be tested this way.
- 362 distinct days. At ~1 filtered signal per day this is a small book however it is cut.

================ CONSEQUENCE ================
The pin-bar family is not dead, but its name was wrong. It is not a 20-SMA pin-bar
strategy — that component fails its own control. It is an ORDER-FLOW strategy that uses
the pin bar to locate candidate bars. Any future work should treat the delta condition as
the thesis and the candle as the scanner.
