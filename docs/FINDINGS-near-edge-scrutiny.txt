================ WHAT THE TRADE ACTUALLY IS ================
  target distance, in POINTS: median 1.48, mean 3.15, 25th pct 0.53, 75th pct 3.84
  stop distance: 30.00 points fixed. Median hold: 2 minute.
  So the trade is: risk 30 points to make 1.5, and it resolves inside one minute 50% of the time.
  The 0.5-point cost assumption is 34% of the gross target.

================ WHY THE GATE TRIPPED ================
  Sharpe 3.61 with a 95% win rate, a 2.4-point target and a 30-point stop is the classic
  pennies-in-front-of-a-steamroller shape. 1,249 wins of +$53 against 57 losses of -$594.
  The t-statistics look strong (pooled 5.5) but they rest on only 57 loss events in ten years,
  so the tail is barely sampled and the t-statistic overstates the confidence.

================ VERDICT ================
NOT A PASS, and not eligible to be one.

1. This is a POST-HOC sweep. The pre-registration declared three target variants. Seven stop
   distances x three targets is 21 combinations, and this was found by looking. It cannot clear a bar
   it was not tested against. Finding something after sweeping is how the original disaster happened.
2. The reality gate TRIPS on Sharpe 3.61.
3. The trade is: risk 30 points to make a MEDIAN OF 1.5 points, resolved within two minutes, winning
   95% of the time. The 0.5-point cost assumption is 34% of the gross target.
4. Cost sensitivity is decisive. Entry is a MARKET order at the 5m close, so it pays the spread both
   ways plus commission - realistically 0.75 to 1.25 points all in on NQ, worse on MNQ. At 1.25 the
   edge is negative on 2017-19 and 0.003 on 2020-22. The whole result lives inside the difference
   between an assumed cost and a real one.
5. Money at a survivable size: about $12 a session on one contract. Scaling it means resting size on a
   1.5-point target, which is precisely where slippage takes the rest.

The honest summary: the wide-stop near-edge variant is a real statistical artefact of a tiny target
and a huge stop, not an edge. It is the same fair-bet arithmetic in a different costume - the win rate
climbs to 95% only because the target shrinks to 1.5 points.
