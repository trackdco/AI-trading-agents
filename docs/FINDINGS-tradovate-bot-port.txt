WHAT THE NUMBERS SHOW
  * It trades enormously. 53,383 trades on 1-minute over 950 sessions is 56 a day. Median hold 5
    minutes. At that rate cost is everything, and 1-minute loses -0.05 R/trade before the stress test
    and -0.12 after: roughly -2,600R over the sample. This is a cost-destruction machine.
  * The pattern is monotone in timeframe, exactly as predicted: 1m -0.05, 5m -0.01, 15m +0.004. The
    strategy is not adding value at any speed; it is simply paying less rent as it slows down.
  * Win rate sits at 42-47% throughout with a median hold of minutes, so the exits (big_drop, pb,
    is_out - any one of which fires) cut trades constantly. That is why it never reaches a target.
  * 2017-19 is the worst tape at every timeframe (-0.115 on 1m), consistent with the tick-screen law
    found earlier in this repository.

  So the bug was accidentally protective. Prediction P1 said the two variants would differ by less
