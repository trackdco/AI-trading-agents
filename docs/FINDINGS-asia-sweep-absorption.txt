THE FINDING THAT MATTERS
  The footprint data was used properly for the first time and it made the strategy WORSE, not better.
  Absorption as defined - heavy aggressive volume at the swept level that fails to move price - does
  not mark a better reversal. It marks a worse one. That is a real, useful negative result about the
  data source, not just about this setup: the extra information did not pay.
