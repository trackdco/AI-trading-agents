READ THIS (corrected - my first explanation was wrong):

The Bollinger band is NOT sitting on top of the VWAP envelope. It is much NARROWER - about 0.43x the
width - and it sits well INSIDE it. The upper Bollinger band is typically 30-45 points BELOW the upper
edge of the respected envelope.

So the real cause is a circularity in my own rule, not the band geometry:
  * I required price to be OUTSIDE the respected VWAP envelope at the signal candle's close.
  * I set the target to the NEAR EDGE of that same envelope.
Those two conditions together guarantee a tiny target. "Just outside the line" and "the line" are the
same place. Whatever the excursion did beforehand, the trade is entered at the moment price is barely
past the edge, so the distance left to travel is ~1.5 points by construction.

The Bollinger break-and-return is almost irrelevant in my build, because the Bollinger band is so far
inside the envelope that price re-enters it long before the envelope filter is satisfied.

The fix is to require a MINIMUM DEPTH beyond the envelope edge before entering, so there is a real
distance to travel back. That is a structural repair of a circular rule, not a parameter tune - but it
is a different strategy and needs its own pre-registration.

VERDICT ON THE DEPTH FIX
Requiring a real excursion does exactly what it should to the target: median distance goes from 2.4
points at depth 0 to 8.6 at depth 10, 17 at depth 20, 23 at depth 30. So the circularity is confirmed
and repaired - the tiny target WAS an artefact of my rule, not of the idea.

But the edge goes with it. 2020-22 flips negative the moment a real target is required (-0.015 at
depth 10, -0.064 at 20, -0.083 at 30) and 2017-19 flips negative too and then runs out of trades
entirely. Only 2023-26 stays positive. Depth 50 looks strong (+0.17 / +0.04) on 15 and 10 trades -
that is not a sample, it is anecdote.

So the honest reading: the near-edge result at depth 0 was a 1.5-point scalp created by a circular
rule, and once the rule is fixed so the trade has somewhere to go, the effect disappears on two of
three tapes. That matches the earlier diagnostic, which found no drift back toward the envelope at any
horizon on any tape. The reversion is not there; only the scalp was, and the scalp dies on costs.

Idea closed. Six tested, six closed.
