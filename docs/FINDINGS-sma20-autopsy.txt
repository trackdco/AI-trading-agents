THREE THINGS WORTH SAYING OUT LOUD
1. The fib result is the OPPOSITE of the video. 0.618 does nothing, 0.5 flips sign
   between tapes, and 0.382 - the level the video says to ignore - is the one that
   shows up. Given 5 fib levels were tested, one clearing by luck is expected, so
   treat 0.382 as unproven rather than as a discovery.
2. Being NEAR the prior-day POC is BAD, not good. Consistent on all three tapes.
   That is the opposite of how POC is normally used as confluence.
3. The single best chart feature is a SMALL signal bar relative to ATR. A big
   dramatic pin bar is worse than a quiet one.

================ HOW BIAS IS BEST IDENTIFIED ================
Four definitions were tested as the trend gate (see FINDINGS from stage 2):
  SIDE  (12 of the last 15 closes on one side of the 20 SMA) ..... best
  HHHL  (last two swing highs AND swing lows both rising) ........ second
  SLOPE (SMA above its own value 10 bars back) ................... third
  NONE  (no filter at all) ....................................... worst
Simple "which side of the SMA has price been living on" beats swing-structure
counting. Both beat nothing, so the trend gate is doing real work.

================ STACKING, AND WHY IT IS NOT PROOF ================
Discovery, small signal bar + VIX <= 20:

Holdout 2017-19:
  raw -0.026 (n=69)    ->  filtered +0.061 (n=21, t +0.15)
The holdout has 21 trades. The sign is right and that is all that can be said.

With order flow joined (337 trades, 2023-26 footprint days only):
  raw                            n=337  +0.043  t +0.44  win 27.0%
  order flow (absorb + bar delta) n=153  +0.202  t +1.34  win 31.4%
  small signal bar               n=189  +0.189  t +1.40  win 31.2%
  orderflow + small bar          n= 90  +0.227  t +1.15  win 32.2%
  orderflow + small bar + calm   n= 76  +0.304  t +1.39  win 34.2%

The filters ARE independent - pairwise correlations between them run -0.02 to +0.29 -
so in principle they stack. But every stacked cell has t around 1.3. Stacking three
filters onto 337 trades leaves 76, and 76 trades cannot settle anything.

================ VERDICT ================
Order flow (stage 3) remains the strongest single filter and it works mainly by
identifying what to avoid. The chart confluences add a real but small tilt, led by
"small signal bar" and proximity to prior-day VAL / prior-day low / opening-range
high. Fib levels are close to worthless here and the two the video recommends are
the two that do least.

What is NOT established: any of the stacked results. They need either more footprint
days or a walk-forward on the 1,290-trade chart-only book.
