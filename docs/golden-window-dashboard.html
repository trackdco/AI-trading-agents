What "gated" means

Golden window, raw 29 trades → what the gate does.

  • CUT 12
    Hollow / below-value. −$1,710 at 17% win. Positive CVD — price poked the level, flow didn't back it.
  • KEEP 17
    Absorption-confirmed. +$5,779 at 53% win. Negative CVD — flow leaning into the level.
  • NET
    Fewer trades, more money, higher win — the gate throws away the coin-flips. That's the whole idea.

CVD is a selectivity lever, not a P&L multiplier: the +$1,030 over single-cap is mostly the extra golden trades in strong months; the win-rate jump is where the real value sits.
