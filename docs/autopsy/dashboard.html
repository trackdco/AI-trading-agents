The two profiles

Your actual question: what do winners share, what do losers share. Everything below is verified out-of-fit.

Winners share

  • The fill bar is with them. Entry-minute delta agrees with direction — the single most universal trait. 35→38% WR vs 17→20%
  • No depth behind the entry. Filled at the edge of the visible book, liquidity ahead on the target side. 35→42% WR, +$64→+$164/t
  • The book builds into the fill (5-min thickness rising). +11pp both years
  • Entered on the with-trade side of daily VWAP and the overnight range — aligned, not stretched. B: 31→40% vs 19→28%
  • Room to breathe: stops above ~7 pts. Win rate is monotone in stop size. 16→33% / 13→31% by quartile
  • Window-correct CVD: pre-market CVD behind pre-window fills; London flow behind golden-window fills.
  • Golden extras: last-15-min tape agreeing (42/31% vs 20/19%); overnight extreme printed hours ago; quiet, two-sided overnight.

Losers share

  • Fill-bar delta against them. 17→20% WR; pre bottom-quartile ≈ 7% WR
  • Depth resting behind the entry — the crowd stacked underneath. 22% WR, −$87→−$98/t
  • Fighting a heavy 15-min tape, worst in the golden window. 21→10% WR, −$189→−$271/t vs avoiders
  • Deep counter-VWAP-side entries (bottom quartile of direction-signed distance). 19→16% WR; B: 15→19%
  • Tiny stops ≤ 6.75 pts. 17→12% WR
  • Strong session CVD opposed (overnight / Asia / London flow against the trade). B: 16→22% WR, −$103→−$268/t
  • Golden extras: chasing the with-direction extreme (−$177→−$240/t); big one-sided overnight flow; both London extremes already swept (−$332→−$115/t).
  • B2 on the 2-min TF (12→16% WR) and B2 into a loud tape.

What happens next

The rulebook is frozen as of this page. Tier-1 effects (wall-behind, fill-delta confirmation, window-correct CVD, geometry alignment, stop-size floor) are large, universal, and mechanistically sensible — trusted now. Tier-2 (frozen combos, quality-score ≥4, thin cells) waits for the holdout: ~100 random trading days of 2023 + ~100 of 2024 (footprint 18:00→10:30 + depth 08:00–10:30, matching existing schemas). That data gets touched once, as a pure test — no re-tuning — which is what makes it validation instead of more fitting. Random sampling beats contiguous months: no regime clustering.

Where this feeds: the fresh-eyes re-run — agent starts every day willing to trade, both books, both windows, no session-level trade/no-trade call. It qualifies each setup live (taxonomy → structural retest → these confirmations) and stands down only because nothing qualified. A 9:40 re-read opens the golden window with its own inputs: pre-market behavior, opening drive, live CVD/footprint, book state.
