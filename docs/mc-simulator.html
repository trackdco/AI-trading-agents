Reading it

The danger zone is the left edge: before the floor locks, a $2k trailing leash sits under a book whose normal annual drawdown is ~$2.2k at 10 micros. Buffer scaling sizes off available drawdown itself — every $1k of headroom adds a step, and the same rule steps size back down as the buffer thins, which is why busting all but disappears: approaching the floor mechanically shrinks the bets that could reach it. Withdrawals shrink the buffer too, so payouts automatically de-risk the account. Paths are resampled from our own backtest days: sequencing risk, not model risk. Trailing is tracked on trade-close equity; funders that trail unrealized intraday would clip these rates slightly.
