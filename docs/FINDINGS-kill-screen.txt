KILL SCREEN — every strategy in the repo against five hard filters
Run 2026-09-08. Filters, applied in order:
  KILL if PF < 1.3 | MaxDD > 25% | trades < 100 | avg win/avg loss < 1.0
  KILL if win rate > 80% AND avg win/avg loss < 1.2   (overfitting flag)

================ METHOD, AND ITS LIMITS ================
Profit factor and percentage drawdown are NOT recorded anywhere in this repo. Neither is
derivable from the summary tables alone. They were produced two ways:

1. EXACT, from per-trade R (data/debug/*.csv, 41 configs + 24 partial-scheme configs):
     PF   = sum(positive R) / |sum(negative R)|
     W/L  = mean(positive R) / |mean(negative R)|
     DD   = max peak-to-trough of the cumulative R curve
     DD%  = DD in R x 1%, ASSUMING 1% of account risked per trade.
   The DD% filter is therefore a function of position sizing, not of the strategy. At 0.5%
   risk every DD% figure halves and more strategies clear the 25% gate. This is the weakest
   of the five filters and should not be read as a property of the edge.

2. DERIVED, for summary-only strategies with FIXED-R exits (11 configs):
     PF = (win x R_target) / ((1 - win) x 1)
   Valid only where every winner books the same multiple. Strategies with level-based or
   trailing exits (variable realised RR) are NOT scoreable this way and are marked approx
   or excluded.

Files: data/debug/kill_metrics_pertrade.csv, kill_metrics_partials.csv, kill_metrics_derived.csv

================ THE OLDER PROGRAMME: EXCLUDED ON DATA VALIDITY ================
The armed-empire family — the 15m BB/MA reject books (A first-of-fight, A+S1, B re-enter),
val break, vwap_m1 break, the val+vwap union, the composite book, the per-session London /
NY_PRE / NY_AM books, and ~40 filter and sizing variants — is excluded WHOLESALE, not by
the five filters.

Reason: every number in those files predates the fill-bar lookahead fix. The corrected
re-run (docs/FINDINGS-exit-next-bar-rerun.txt) gives negative mean R on all three tapes and
in every one of ten years:
    2023-26 armed -0.1833 R/trade | flat -0.1626
    2020-22 armed -0.1748         | flat -0.1474
    2017-19 armed -0.1498         | flat -0.1359
Negative mean R means gross profit < gross loss, so PF < 1 by definition for all of them.
Any file in that family still reading "ALIVE" is quoting the superseded column.
