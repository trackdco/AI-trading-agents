================ VERDICT ================
All three pre-registered conditions PASS. The idea still fails, and the reason is the comparison the
pre-registration did not name: **buy and hold beats overnight-only on every tape, on both raw points
and Sharpe.** Overnight-only +9.34 pts/day at Sharpe 0.86 against +17.74 at Sharpe 1.09 on 2023-26;
same ordering on 2020-22 and 2017-19.

So there is no separable overnight premium to harvest. Both legs are positive because the index rose
for ten years. Per hour of exposure the DAY session pays 1.7x to 4.0x more than the overnight session,
which is the opposite of the hypothesis. Overnight's better Sharpe comes from lower volatility (fewer
hours, less news), not from a bigger payment for holding risk. And overnight's worst single day was
SMALLER than the day session's on all three tapes, so the gap-risk story does not show up either.

Two of three predictions were wrong, which is the useful part: the story told about WHY this should
work was not the story in the data.

What this is, stated plainly: leveraged long NQ, sliced by clock. Not an edge. A pass on a
pre-registered bar that was itself too weak - the bar should have required beating buy and hold.
LESSON RECORDED: any long-only test must clear buy-and-hold on both return and risk-adjusted return,
declared in advance. Added to docs/REALITY-GATE.md.
