================ A CORRECTION TO MY OWN SCORING ================
The addendum's third condition — "win rate clears the 33.3% break-even for the 1:2 target"
— is MIS-SPECIFIED and my first pass reported "0 of 5 markets pass" on the strength of it.
That is wrong and withdrawn. This book has THREE outcomes, not two: 19-31% of trades exit
on the 120-minute timer, and those FLAT exits average +0.36 to +0.53 R. A nominal 1:2
target therefore does not imply a 33.3% break-even. The correct break-even uses the
realised average win and average loss. Re-scored below on that basis.

================ THE CAVEAT THAT MATTERS MOST ================
NQ, RTY, YM and ES are ALL US equity index futures and are heavily correlated. This is
closer to 1.5 independent tests than to four. Gold is the only structurally different
market in the set, and gold is where the rule dies.

The honest statement is NOT "the rule works across markets". It is "the rule works on US
equity index futures and not on metals". That is still worth having - three separate
contracts, and it says something about what behaviour the rule captures - but it is one
asset class.

UNEXPLAINED LOOSE THREAD: ES is the weak one. It is the most liquid equity index and the
most correlated with NQ, so it should look most like NQ. It does not (+0.069 versus
+0.158, and its first half is negative). No explanation for this yet.
