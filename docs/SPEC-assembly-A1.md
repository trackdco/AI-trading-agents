## Components and how they combine — fixed now

1. **Book**: M2 REJECT arm only. (The arm every study measured; break-retest
   is deferred whole to a future A-2 with its own blind spec — it does NOT
   get grafted on later because its numbers look good.)
2. **Entry**: mechanical, at the failing 15m bar's close. No discretion leg
   in A-1.
3. **Stop**: trigger-candle extreme ±1 tick. **Exit**: hold-with-stop to
   17:00 — the shipped convention. No exit swaps, no trail revival.
4. **Sizing = the only place selection enters, as WEIGHTS not gates.**
   Conviction rank = causal percentile of `path_emptiness` against the
   trailing 60 sessions' event distribution (no within-era rank — that was
   in-sample; live must rank against the past only). Rank quartiles map to
   the EXISTING 0.5/1.0/1.5/2.0 ladder, untuned.
5. **bw_expansion clause, resolved by a query this spec has not seen:**
   if |corr(path_emptiness, bw_expansion)| < 0.4 on fit, the conviction rank
   is the MEAN of the two causal percentiles; if >= 0.4 they are treated as
   one variable and `path_emptiness` alone is used. Declared before the
   correlation is known.
6. **Fresh-breaks-only / admissibility: EXCLUDED from A-1 sizing.** It
   failed its census bar; it enters as a reported overlay column only.
   Recorded here so it cannot be quietly promoted later.
7. **Sessions**: evaluated on ALL sessions (the law: tagged, never filtered);
   the HEADLINE cells are ny_pre + ny_rth_am (the desk's window). Asia/London
   reported as expansion candidates with the thin-liquidity caveat standing.
