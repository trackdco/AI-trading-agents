Correction to the note above: the stop is roughly 1.0-1.4x the median bar range, not half it - but the

end the trade, and 55-60% of trades die on bar one. Median hold is a single 3-minute bar.

Three diagnostics, none of which rescue it:
  * FIRST BREAK ONLY (the classic ORB, rather than their re-entering loop): still negative on all

  * A STOP 4x WIDER (2.0 x ATR instead of 0.5): median hold rises from 3 to ~45 minutes and the losses

WHAT THIS SAYS ABOUT THE SOURCE REPO
  Nothing dishonest - it publishes no performance claim, and its exit design measurably outperforms
  the fixed-target alternatives. What is untested by this run is its ML entry grader, which is the
  entire point of the system: the mechanical entries are meant to be FILTERED down to the graded few,
  not all taken. Those models ship pre-trained with no training script, so the filter cannot be
  audited or reproduced here. The honest statement is: the raw ORB entry is a loser on 3-minute bars
  across five instruments and five years, and whether the grader turns it into a winner is unknown
  and unverifiable from this repository.
