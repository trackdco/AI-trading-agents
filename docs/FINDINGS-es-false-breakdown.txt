================ WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT SAY ================
DOES: the rule as literally written - break a prior-day / overnight / multi-hour low,
reclaim it, hold above it for two bars, stop under the dip, target 10-15 - has no edge on
five years of ES. It is a fair bet that loses the spread. Every stop size, every target,
every level, both halves.

DOES NOT: prove the poster is wrong about their own results. Their edge, if real, is
entirely in the parts that were not written down - which level to trust, which reclaims
look right, which to skip. That is consistent with what they said themselves: "takes lots
of reps to get right." A mechanical version cannot inherit that, and this test shows the
mechanical skeleton on its own is worth nothing.

Footnote: the claim itself trips our own reality gate. 60% win at 1:1.3 is +16.5 points
above break-even; the gate hard-fails anything over +15. That check is calibrated for
BACKTESTS, where such a number means a lookahead bug. A live discretionary record is a
different kind of evidence and the gate does not apply to it. But it does say the claim
sits at the edge of what a backtest would ever be allowed to assert.
