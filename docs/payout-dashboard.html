Read this honestly

  • Resamples our own 82 winning 2026 trades. The pass/payout %s prove the sizing clears the DD math under normal variance — not that the edge repeats on live fills. That's the real risk and no MC can price it.
  • Two phases multiply. Even at ~99.6% each, needing both drops the combined to 99.3%. Sequential gates compound against you.
  • Expected $3,970/attempt is a floor — it counts only reaching the full $4k. In practice you withdraw along the way, so realized EV is higher; but it also ignores the eval fee and any consistency rule.
  • Sizing 8/4/4 (HIGH 23 / MID 44 / LOW 15 trades) sits between the safe 6/3/3 and the thin 10/5/5 — 95%-DD ~$1,502 still inside the $2k buffer.
