## Integrity event — a leak I caused, found, and corrected
A bug in my scan helper measured MFE over a six-hour window instead of the trade's life,
producing a phantom high of 30,968 for NYP1. I copied that into the 09:30 and 09:44
briefings as `session_high` — a price not printed until **10:10**. The 09:30 thesis
reasoned from it.

The whole NY_AM chain was **voided and re-run** on corrected as-of briefings; the voided
rows are preserved at `superseded/2026-06-21_voided-nyam-chain.jsonl`.

**The material cost is the clearest demonstration of why the gate matters:** the
contaminated chain flipped short at 10:02 and took the 10:16 rejection for **+2.92R**
(price then collapsed 373pt). The clean chain, given only as-of data, stays long and
**passes** it. One leaked price was the difference between a 2.92R trade and no trade —
which is also a caution against reading the voided number as skill.

**Audit gap found:** `audit_run_leak` did not catch it. Check C validates *times* in
briefings; check E validates *prices* in decision rows. A future **price** inside a
**briefing** is checked by neither. E should be extended to scan briefing bodies against
the as-of printed range.
