Pre-handover findings — small adjustments before the agents run FROM THE DATA

  • 1. Adopt volatility-normalized units — RESOLVED, measured. Vol-scaling beats price-scaling everywhere and recovers $1.6–5.9k/arm in 2023 at zero 2026 cost. All point-params become vol-ratios in the agent-era config. Stage B (normalizing detection-level cluster tolerance, needs one re-detection) queued.
  • 2. Demote the v1.1 cuts from champion to regime-conditional. C1/C2/C3 added +$4k in 2026 and subtracted real money in 2024. They were journal-mined on one era. Proposal: cuts become candidate rules the agents may apply when analogs support them — not hardwired law. (C1 likely survives normalization as a ratio; C2/C3 must re-earn their place.)
  • 3. Give the replay baseline BOTH raw books + the switch, not just v1.1. The 2024 data shows raw books beat the cut-down champion off-era — agents should inherit the toolbox, not one era's favorite configuration.
  • 4. The event calendar is the cheapest big win. Aug-2024 (carry unwind) and Nov-2024 (election week) are exactly the months a news-reading agent converts from disasters to ≈0. A historical headline archive (GDELT, free) + scheduled-event calendar for 2023–25 is required for the replay's news arm — on Brake's list.
  • 5. Depth study stays April 2026 (full month being purchased): every engine trade + every Angus live execution gets its book state reconstructed at entry — the loser-cutting layer's exam.
  • 6. Gate agent authority by library depth. The agents' predicted worst failure mode is overconfident calls on thin analogs (the L2 benchmark already showed a shallow library losing to the dumb switch). Rule for Pat: below N comparable analog days, the agent may only stand down or defer to mechanical — never override with conviction. Authority is earned as the library deepens.
  • 7. 2025 is the agents' real exam. A year where both books bleed, the day-type index reads normal, and the oracle stays green 12/12 — pure decision-layer territory. If the agent arm can't at least go flat-ish through 2025, the stack isn't ready; if it turns 2025 green, it's real.
