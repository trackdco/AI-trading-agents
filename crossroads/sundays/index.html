What to expect

How long
About 90 minutes, then morning tea or supper.
What happens
We sing, pray, hear the Bible read and explained, and spend time together.
Kids and teens
Crèche, Kids@Church and Youth@Church run at the two morning services, North and Belconnen.
After church
Morning tea at North and Belconnen, supper at City, and dinner together at Lake G.
New here
Look for someone wearing a Crossroads lanyard, and fill in a Connect Card so we can be in touch.
Venues do change from time to time, and over Christmas and New Year the usual times and places don’t apply. If you’re planning a visit around then, check our Facebook page or email office@crossroads.asn.au first.
