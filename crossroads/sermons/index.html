Sermons

There are times when getting to church in person is hard. If you’re unwell, travelling, or away from Canberra, you can still keep listening along.

This week and every week

Each week we upload the sermons from across our services, so you can revisit a talk or catch up if you couldn’t be there.

The sermon library
