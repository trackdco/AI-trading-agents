Crossroads English

Free classes for speakers of other languages who want to have fun and improve their English. Conversational, real-life English, and help getting to know and love Canberra.

What we do

  • Free English classes that focus on the practical speaking and listening skills people use most.
  • A supportive, caring learning community and social network for people who have left family and friends.
  • Help finding new networks in Canberra.
  • Help getting to know and enjoy Canberra’s opportunities and facilities.
  • Help understanding and joining in with Australian culture and customs.
  • Help understanding why we do what we do.

Why we do it

We love Jesus, and he loves people who have left homes and families to live in new places. He wants his family to help people in need who come to their community.

When and where

Classes
Weekly, intermediate level, during school terms: Tuesdays, 7pm to 9pm.
Also
Lunch and dinner get-togethers in our family homes, and special events to enjoy Canberra’s community celebrations, festivals and programs together.
Where
Crossroads Ministry Centre, Level 1, 8 Chandler Street, Belconnen ACT 2617. Check the Facebook page for where we are currently meeting.
Cost
Free.
