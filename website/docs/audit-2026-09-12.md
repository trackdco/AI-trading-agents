# Site audit, 12 September 2026

End-to-end audit of the Imperium Detailing site after the SEO, speed, mobile and conversion pass.
Everything below was measured on the static export, served locally without compression.
Real hosting (Vercel, with Brotli and HTTP/2) will score higher than these numbers, not lower.

## How it was tested

- Lighthouse 12, mobile (simulated slow 4G, 4x CPU slowdown) and desktop presets.
- axe-core (WCAG 2.1 A and AA plus best practice) on all 30 pages, at 360px and 1280px wide.
- Horizontal overflow check on all 30 pages at 360, 768 and 1280px.
- Metadata check on all 30 pages: title length, description length, one H1, heading order, image alt and size attributes, internal links, canonical, JSON-LD validity.
- Browser console errors on every page at three widths.

SEO 100, accessibility 100 and best practices 93 on every page tested, before and after.
The missing best-practice points come from the ad and chat scripts being blocked in the test sandbox, and from testing over plain HTTP.

- Layout shift: 0 on every page.
- Accessibility: 0 axe violations on 30 pages at both widths.
- Overflow: none at 360, 768 or 1280px on any page.
- Metadata: every page has a unique title under 60 characters and a description between 70 and 160 characters, one H1, no heading jumps, all images with alt and size, no broken internal links, a canonical, and valid JSON-LD (64 blocks).
- Console: no page errors.

## What changed

### Speed

- Replaced the animation library behind the reviews marquee with CSS (142 KB of JavaScript gone), and cut the marquee from 45 cards to 15. DOM size went from 1,347 elements to 878.
- The quote form no longer imports a component library for its inputs.
- Video posters converted from JPEG to WebP: 1.2 MB became 385 KB across six files. Logos converted the same way.
- Hero video re-encoded: 27 percent smaller on phones, 20 percent smaller on desktop, no visible change.
- The hero poster is preloaded at high priority, the hero video no longer downloads twice (once as a fallback source, once at the chosen size), and the blurred backdrop only renders on desktop.
- Carousel videos don't preload; only the card in the middle plays.
- Scroll effects wait for the first idle moment, so the page paints and hydrates first.
- The film grain overlay is off on phones. The phone bar no longer uses a backdrop blur.
- The chat widget loads only on screens 768px and wider, where it doesn't sit on the phone bar.
- A Vercel config caches images, media and brand files for a week.

### SEO

- Titles shortened on 8 pages, descriptions fixed on 11.
- Breadcrumbs (visible plus BreadcrumbList data) on service, area, guide, maintenance and EV pages.
- Service schema on each service page. Opening hours corrected to 8:30am to 5:30pm, every day.
- Internal linking: "Keep reading" and "Also useful" blocks on every service page, an EV row on the services page, related links on the maintenance and EV pages, and the new pages in the footer and sitemap.

### Mobile

- The header tucks away while scrolling down and returns when scrolling up.
- Text and Call stay pinned to the bottom of the screen, stepping aside for the quote form and footer buttons.
- The price guide scrolls to the answer once a job is picked.
- Footer links have more room around them for thumbs.

### Conversions

- Real prices for every vehicle size, including motorbikes, in the instant price guide, with a text message pre-filled with the job and price.
- Service pages pre-pick their service in the quote form. Links can do the same with `/book/?service=Ceramic%20coating`.
- "What happens next" in three steps beside the form, plus the 24-hour notice and opening hours.
- Every call, text and form send fires a Google Ads and Meta event, tagged with the section it came from.

### Motion

- Buttons lift on hover. The new price settles in. Answers open softly. Gallery and service photos lean in on hover. All transform and opacity only, all off under reduced motion.

## Friction points still open

1. **The quote form sends a text, not a lead into the CRM.** Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_FORM_ENDPOINT` to the LeadConnector webhook. Until then, desktop visitors see the message to copy.
2. **Google Ads conversion labels.** Events fire, but Google Ads needs a conversion action per event to count them. Create actions for `contact_call`, `contact_text` and `generate_lead`.
3. **Meta Pixel ID** is empty. Add it and the same events reach Meta.
4. **Self-booking.** Detailing jobs could book straight into Urable. Add the public booking link and the "Lock it in" buttons can point at it for detail jobs.
5. **Instagram posts** need post codes; the strip shows one reel until then.
6. **A photo of the two of you** on the home page. The biggest missing trust signal for a service done at someone's home.
7. **Review count** is fixed at 45 and 4.9. Update it every month or two.

## Known limits

- The remaining JavaScript (about 230 KB unused on first load) is React and the Next.js runtime. Removing it means a different framework.
- The display font has no automatic fallback metrics, so the fallback font differs slightly for a moment on slow connections. Layout shift measured 0 regardless.
- Instagram embeds and the chat widget are blocked in the preview host. Both work on the real domain.
