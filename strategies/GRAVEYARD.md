# The Graveyard

Strategies that failed the gate. Kept deliberately.

Two reasons this file earns its place:

1. **It stops us re-testing the same idea in six months** when it resurfaces on
   YouTube with a new name. Most "new" retail strategies are old ones relabelled.
2. **The pattern of failures is itself information.** If six candidates in a row
   die at "degradation ratio", we have a process problem — we're over-refining —
   not six unlucky strategies.
