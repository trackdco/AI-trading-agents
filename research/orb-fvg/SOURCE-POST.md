## The performance claim, as posted

> "nearly $20K this month across my prop accounts"

`[trader-claimed, unverified]`

**Why this claim carries close to zero evidential weight, stated once so it is not re-litigated:**

1. **One month.** A single month is not a sample; it is one draw.
2. **"Across my prop accounts" is multiplication, not replication.** The same trades taken on
   *n* accounts produce *n* × the dollars and **one** independent result. Dollar totals scale with
   account count and size; they say nothing about win rate, expectancy, or R.
3. **No denominator.** No account count, no account size, no trade count, no losing month, no
   drawdown, no starting balance.
4. **Selection is unobservable.** A month that was not posted is a month we do not see.

The dollar figure is therefore recorded and **never used as evidence for or against anything**.
