## Notable for the team

- **Macro windows are demoted here.** He says they are "the highest probability… time zones"
  `[@ 01:04]` but immediately: *"just trading the full session completely eliminates the fact
  that you missed those trades"* `[@ 01:26]`. Anyone testing "ICT macro windows" as a hard
  filter would be testing something he does not do.
- **ES is a required second instrument.** The model's confirmation leg lives on ES
  `[@ 05:47, 07:40, 09:14]`. Any NQ-only reconstruction is a strictly weaker model than
  taught — and this repo holds no ES data.
- **Three rules are explicitly discretionary** (stop variant, trailing, "internal" sweeps),
  and he says the judgement cannot be taught `[@ 04:42]`.
