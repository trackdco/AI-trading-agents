# nypre-0830-event-tree — the 8:30 print as a two-branch event system

## Thesis (for Angus)

CPI, jobs, claims land at 08:30 — inside your live session — and the moment
splits every such morning into two tradeable branches. HOLD: a first-minute
move big enough (multiples of pre-release vol) to be a true surprise can't be
fully repositioned in a minute — cash accounts and open-benchmarked funds queue
behind it, so enter the impulse direction on the first shallow pullback after
08:35; if the reaction is still holding at 09:15, the opening flow tends to
extend it (news-day momentum is a published result). FAIL: an impulse that
breaks the pre-market extreme and closes back inside within minutes was a stop
raid into a deliberately thinned book — fade it toward the pre-release range.
The wrong side, respectively: the reflexive spike-fader, and the breakout
chaser. Important honesty: the naive unconditional version is PUBLISHED DEAD on
our own instrument (the falsification study) — this candidate exists entirely
in the conditioning: magnitude gate, PM-extreme interaction, pullback entries,
minutes 4–60 not the HFT seconds. A tier-2 arm (claims/PPI with a higher gate)
tests whether low-attention shocks digest slower.

## Skeleton

Tier-1 days: R = |08:30→08:31| vs same-slot baseline. R ≥ k× → branch by
PM-extreme interaction: break-and-hold → continuation entry post-08:35, target
measured move into 09:30 (M5 variant holds to ≤10:00); poke-and-fail → fade to
PM mid. CVD absorption on the pullback is the strongest filter. No-release
days: no trade.
