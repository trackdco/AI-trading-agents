`orochi-credibility.md`. Everything below is labelled VERIFIED (fetched

## 2. WHAT HE SELLS, AND WHO PAYS HIM

**VERIFIED:**

  **$99.99/mo** (live alerts, daily streams), **$149.99/mo** "Premium"
  (adds education library + session recordings), **$1,999 lifetime**.
- **He is building his own prop-firm rewards/cashback marketplace,
  SmartPropFirm** ("Buy props. Get rewarded"; "Get paid out → receive a
  free evaluation on us; earn points with every purchase — redeem them
  for more challenges"). The promo code is embedded in his video
  descriptions.
- Site lists four prop-firm "Trusted Partners": **ApexTraderFunding,
  Tradeify, TakeProfit, TraderLucid**.
- A third-party paid invite-only TradingView script named "B7lanto"
  exists under publisher `basheer1414` with WhatsApp-payment access. No
  evidence it is his; reads as name-riding on his brand. Noted, not
  attributed.

**The structural conflict, stated plainly.** Thraxx had an affiliate
link; **Lanto owns the funnel.** His revenue scales with viewers buying
prop-firm evaluations through his own platform, and prop firms make money
when evaluations fail. Every +$100k-in-an-hour title is, functionally, an
advertisement for evaluation purchases routed through his code. **This
does not make his mechanics wrong — mechanics are what we test — but it
disqualifies every P&L claim on the channel from being treated as
evidence.** Same standing rule as the Thraxx intake, with a stronger
incentive gradient.

## 3. THE PERFORMANCE CLAIMS

**VERIFIED (that the claims were made, not that they are true):** recent
titles claim +$90k, +$130k (1 trade), +$51k in 10 minutes, +$64k in 1
hour, +$100k live "in front of 1,600+ people", +$224k ("10R trade"),
**+$3,100,000 in a day ("NOT CLICKBAIT")**, +$400k live; channel bio
claims "#1 Live Market Payout in Prop Firm History"; a "What a Max Prop
Firm Payout Actually Looks Like" video exists.

**NOT FOUND:** any third-party verification. For scale: the largest
*verified* single prop payouts on public record are ~$500k (FTMO,
REPORTED) and ~$347k (FundedNext, blockchain-settled, REPORTED). His
claimed daily P&L exceeds these by an order of magnitude — the arithmetic
only works as **simultaneous copy-trading across a large stack of funded
accounts**, which is also the only way "+$130,000 with 1 trade" happens
on NQ at prop risk limits. Nothing wrong with that per se, but it means
the dollar figures measure account count, not edge, and payout-vs-P&L is
never disambiguated in titles.

**The counter-evidence is real and worth weighing**, same as Thraxx: he
publishes losses prominently and by title (−$79k "ROOKIE MISTAKE", −$73k,
−$41k "1 tick from TP", −$40k "SLOPPY", −$34k "psychology was off"), and
he demonstrably trades live on schedule in front of a paying audience.
Critical member feedback found in reviews: sometimes late to the morning
session; **"holds on to trades even though there is no chance of them
ending in profit"** — an execution-discipline observation from his own
customers, worth remembering if his management rules are ever tested.
