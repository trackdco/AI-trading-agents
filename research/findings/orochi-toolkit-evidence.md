# Quantification Survey: Published Evidence for Auction-Market-Theory Toolkit Concepts (NQ Strategy-Validation Program)

**Evidence tiers used below:** [A] peer-reviewed / SSRN working paper with stated methodology · [B] quant-blog / platform backtest with stated sample sizes and mechanical rules · [C] vendor/educator claim with no verifiable methodology (folklore). Anything at tier C only is flagged **FOLKLORE-ONLY**.

**Data layers:** L1 = 1-min OHLCV 2023–2026 · L2 = per-minute footprint delta Jun 2025–Jul 2026 · L3 = order-book heatmap depth Jun 2025–Jul 2026. Note: L2/L3 give only ~270 trading sessions — enough for per-event statistics (hundreds–thousands of events), not enough for regime-robust day-level claims.

## 1. TPO / Market Profile structures

**(a) Mechanical definitions (codable):**
- TPO profile: 30-min letter periods A–M over RTH 9:30–16:00 ET; each price level collects one letter per period that traded it. TPO-POC = price with most letters; Value Area = 70% of TPOs built outward from POC; VPOC = volume-based POC.
- Naked/virgin POC: prior-session POC not yet traded through by any later session.
- Poor/unfinished high (low): 2+ TPOs flat at the session extreme (no single-print excess/tail). "Repair" = later session trades through it.
- Single prints: interior price run with exactly one TPO letter, sandwiched between multi-TPO areas (extremes excluded — those are tails).
- 80% rule: open outside prior value area, then price re-enters and holds inside VA for two consecutive 30-min periods → play for full traverse to other VA extreme. Origin: Dalton Capital Management's *Profile Reports* (1987–1991), popularized via a MarketDelta strategy PDF ([Quantopian archive thread](https://quantopian-archive.netlify.app/forum/threads/how-to-pick-intraday-market-direction-the-80-percent-rule.html)).

**(b) Strongest published quantitative claims:**
- **Single prints [B — best-in-class for this family]:** [TradingStats single-print study](https://tradingstats.net/single-prints-market-profile-research/) — 3,847 zones, NQ/ES/YM/RTY, 1-min data Feb 2014–Mar 2026 (~3,100 days/instrument). Fill rates ~32% D+1, 63–67% by D+5, ~74% by D+10; 14–17% never filled in 30 days; zone width as % of day range is the best fill-speed predictor (small zones 64–70% by D+5 vs 47–50% large); conditional decay: unfilled past D+7 → ever-fill probability drops below 50%. Fading single-print days is bad: full session reversal only 6–10% (stable 2014–2026, NQ yearly range 6.7–14.0%). Cross-checked against a manual [OrderFlow Labs](https://orderflowlabs.com/blogs/theblog/market-profile) count (717 NQ days, 52% frequency vs their algorithmic 52.9%).
- **Prior-value/POC touch probabilities [B]:** [TradingStats session-open study](https://tradingstats.net/session-open-analysis-futures/) — NQ 3,116 / ES 3,042 sessions, 2014–2026: open inside prior value area → prior VPOC touched 82–85%; prior VAH touched → VPOC also touched 81.8% on NQ (1,379/1,686) and 82–84% across instruments; open above value → prior-high touch 71–77%. This is the closest thing to a real, sampled "POC magnet" number in print.
- **80% rule / geometry falsification [B, negative]:** [pedrobraiti/volume-profile-trading](https://github.com/pedrobraiti/volume-profile-trading) — walk-forward OOS on 17–33 years (SPY/QQQ + 3 Brazilian names), costs, volume-permutation/random-entry/ablation falsification. Findings: the 80% rule traversed the VA only **27–67%** of the time (never 80%) and lost after costs; POC-reversion/edge-to-edge/VA-breakout show OOS PF 1.1–2.1 on US indices but **no sleeve survives falsification** — no alpha over long exposure, PF 95% CI includes 1.0. Verdict: "the geometry alone (POC, Value Area, day-types, 80% Rule) is mostly folklore."
- The [Quantopian community replication](https://quantopian-archive.netlify.app/forum/threads/how-to-pick-intraday-market-direction-the-80-percent-rule.html) of the 80% rule (2015-era) never produced a stable win rate — implementations diverged because "trades in VA for two consecutive half-hour periods" is under-specified.

**(c) Criticisms / failure modes:**
- The famous numeric claims are unverifiable vendor copy: "naked POC revisited ~80% within 10 sessions" ([GoCharting](https://gocharting.com/blog/volume-profile/point-of-control-trading-guide)), "78% of poor highs exceeded within 5 sessions" ([TradeAlgo](https://www.tradealgo.com/trading-guides/technical-analysis/market-profile-how-to-understand-value-areas-and-price-distribution)), "80% rule = 80% success on all backtests" ([Vtrender tweet](https://x.com/Am_Shai/status/1680924992421396481)) — all tier C, no samples, no definitions. NexusFi traders who backtested the 80% rule "did not get anywhere close" ([thread](https://nexusfi.com/showthread.php?t=30847)).
- Touch-probability stats need base-rate controls: prior VPOC sits near the middle of yesterday's range, and consecutive-day ranges overlap heavily, so high touch rates are partly mechanical. Test edge = hit-rate minus base rate.
- Zero peer-reviewed literature exists on TPO constructs per se (search confirms no SSRN/journal empirical test of value-area rules).
- **Verdict: single prints and value-area touch rates are PUBLISHED-QUANTIFIED (tier B); naked-POC revisit %, poor-high repair %, and the 80% rule's "80%" are FOLKLORE-ONLY numbers (with a tier-B falsification against the 80% rule).**

**(d) Data needed:** L1 alone suffices for TPO structures, VA/POC (TPO-based), single prints, poor highs. L2 upgrades POC to true VPOC and per-price volume.

## 2. Dalton open types (open-drive / open-test-drive / open-rejection-reverse / open-auction)

**(a) Mechanical definition:** Qualitative in *Mind over Markets* (Dalton, pp. 171–181 of *Markets in Profile* per [ParaCurve](https://paracurve.com/2020/02/day-opening-types.html)). Codable proxies: open-drive = one-directional move from the open that never trades back through the open price (and clears a reference like ON high); open-test-drive = test beyond a reference (prior VA edge / ON high-low), reversal through the open, then drive; open-rejection-reverse = directional move then full reversal back through the opening range; open-auction = rotation around the open with no conviction.

**(b) Strongest published quantitative claims:** **None exist for the taxonomy itself — FOLKLORE-ONLY.** No published frequency or follow-through table for the four open types was found anywhere (searches across academic + practitioner sources). The nearest quantified adjacents:
- Open location (not type): gap-up ~25%, gap-down ~15%, in-range ~60% of sessions; conditional level-touch odds as in family 1 ([TradingStats](https://tradingstats.net/session-open-analysis-futures/), 3,000+ days/instrument).
- Early-direction persistence: first 15-min candle green → day closes green ~70% (SPY, [TradeThatSwing](https://tradethatswing.com/high-probability-stock-market-statistics/)); IB close direction → same-side IB break 74–81% (NQ, 3,116 sessions, [TradingStats IB-VPOC study](https://tradingstats.net/ib-vpoc-research/)).
- The folklore "HOD/LOD forms in first 30/60 min ~50%/75% of the time" ([TradingBalance](https://tradingbalance.co.uk/market-profile-opening-types-and-how-to-use-the-information-to-trade-successfully/), tier C) is contradicted for full 22h sessions by [TradingStats HOD/LOD timing](https://tradingstats.net/hod-lod-timing-research/) (12,095 sessions): the single most common HOD window is the **last** 30 min (15:30, 17.3% NQ); ~73–77% of days put HOD and LOD on opposite sides of noon.

**(c) Criticisms:** the taxonomy is under-specified (how far is a "test"? which reference?), so any test requires you to freeze arbitrary parameters; classification is partly retrospective. Academic mechanism support is limited to U-shaped intraday volume/volatility (Wood/McInish/Ord 1985; Admati & Pfleiderer 1988) — opens genuinely carry outsized information flow, but that doesn't validate the four-way grammar.

**(d) Data needed:** L1; L2 adds a real "conviction" measure (open delta) that Dalton could only eyeball — this is a place your program can generate novel evidence.

## 3. Day types / balance-imbalance (trend days, range extension, b/P profiles)

**(a) Mechanical definitions:** Initial Balance = first-hour range (9:30–10:30). Range extension = trade beyond IB. Trend day (common codable proxy): open and close in opposite deciles of the day range with range > k×ATR and persistent one-directionality. b-shape / P-shape: volume profile with fat node at bottom/top and thin tail on the other end, read as long-liquidation / short-covering respectively.

**(b) Strongest published quantitative claims [B]:**
- [TradingStats IB breakout study](https://tradingstats.net/initial-balance-breakout-statistics/) — ES 2,686 + NQ 2,833 days, 2015–2025, 1-min: at least one IB break on **97.8% (ES) / 96.2% (NQ)** of days; "pure balance" days (IB holds both ends) only 2.2%/3.8%; double break 28.7% ES / 22.6% NQ. Extension ladder (by close): 25% extension ~52% ES / 47% NQ; 100% extension 18.8% up / 20.5% down ES, 12.8% / 15.9% NQ; 200% extension 4.4%/7.1% ES. IB size is the strongest conditioner: narrow IB (<0.5×ATR) breaks 98.7% with median extension 74.8%; extreme IB (>1.5×ATR) breaks 66.7%, median extension 22.3%. IB direction alone predicts break side at 74–81% (adding IB-VPOC position adds **zero** uplift — tested and null, [IB-VPOC study](https://tradingstats.net/ib-vpoc-research/)).
- Trend-day frequency proxy: single-print (one-timeframing) days = 37–53% of sessions, but **full-reversal of a directional day only 6–10%** ([single-print study](https://tradingstats.net/single-prints-market-profile-research/)).
- Negative test of day-type predictiveness [B]: the [volume-profile falsification repo](https://github.com/pedrobraiti/volume-profile-trading) found day-types do **not** predict next-day continuation ("bearish" days bounced more — mild reversion, opposite of the folklore).

**(c) Criticisms / failure modes:** "Trend days are ~10% of days" (Dalton-lineage folklore) has no published source and depends entirely on the chosen definition. b-shape = long liquidation / P-shape = short covering has **zero published statistics** — even [NinjaTrader's explainer](https://ninjatrader.com/futures/blogs/trade-futures-understanding-the-4-common-volume-profile-shapes/) caveats that shape ≠ mechanism; verifying the "short-covering" interpretation requires positioning data (or delta), not shape. **Verdict: range-extension odds are PUBLISHED-QUANTIFIED (tier B, big samples); b/P-shape narratives are FOLKLORE-ONLY.**

**(d) Data needed:** L1 for day typing and IB stats; L2 to test the b/P mechanism claim directly (e.g., is the fat node built on negative delta absorption?) — another novel-evidence opportunity.

## 4. VWAP (bands, magnet, anchored, execution mechanism)

**(a) Mechanical definitions:** session VWAP = Σ(price×volume)/Σvolume from 9:30 (or Globex open — must pick); bands at ±1σ/±2σ where σ = stdev of price deviations from VWAP (or volatility-scaled); anchored VWAP = same accumulation from an arbitrary event bar.

**(b) Strongest published quantitative claims:**
- **Momentum side (strongest evidence, tier A/B):** Zarattini & Aziz, ["VWAP: The Holy Grail for Day Trading Systems"](https://concretumgroup.com/volume-weighted-average-price-vwap-the-holy-grail-for-day-trading-systems/) (SSRN): QQQ 2018–2023, long above VWAP / short below: +671% net, Sharpe 2.1, maxDD 9.4% (vs buy-hold +126%, DD 37%). Zarattini/Aziz/Barbon ["Beat the Market"](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4824172) (SPY 2007–2024, noise-band breakout with VWAP trailing stop): +1,985% net, 19.6% ann., Sharpe 1.33. **Independently replicated on your instrument**: [Quantitativo](https://www.quantitativo.com/p/intraday-momentum-for-es-and-nq) re-implemented it on ES/NQ futures with Databento 1-min data 2010–2025 and realistic costs (0.5 tick/trade slippage): NQ version 24.3% ann., Sharpe 1.67, win rate 38%, payoff 2.25, +6 bps/trade; ES+NQ portfolio Sharpe 1.57; caveats — strategy was flat 2010–2017 and highly slippage-sensitive.
- **Reversion side (tier B, weaker):** QuantConnect 2022 test on 100 liquid NASDAQ stocks (as reported [here](https://www.quantvps.com/blog/backtest-vwap-trading-strategy-python)): fade +2σ band 63% win rate (~1.5:1), −2σ 61%, 3σ touches ~71% reversion. The widely quoted "75–80% reversion at 2σ–3σ" figures ([FuturesHive](https://www.futureshive.com/blog/vwap-trading-strategy-futures-2025) etc.) are tier C. One honest vendor caveat that matches reversion physics: unfiltered band-fade wins ~45% and "fails badly on trend days."
- **Mechanism (tier A, the strongest part):** VWAP is the dominant institutional execution benchmark — Berkowitz, Logue & Noser (JF 1988) founded VWAP benchmarking; Madhavan (2002) "VWAP strategies"; [Białkowski, Darolles & Le Fol (J. Banking & Finance 2008)](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=932699) model intraday volume decomposition specifically to track end-of-day VWAP (CAC40 sample); VWAP algos ≈20% of US equity algo volume (Bloomberg Intelligence, via [TradeAlgo](https://www.tradealgo.com/trading-guides/technical-analysis/vwap-trading-strategy-the-institutional-benchmark-every-trader-should-know)); 74% of hedge funds report using VWAP algos ([ValorAlgo](https://www.valoralgo.com/blog/vwap-trading-doctrine-institutional-guide), survey-tier). Mechanism implication: volume-curve-slaved passive flow genuinely concentrates around VWAP — a real reason for both stickiness (magnet) and why deviations carry information. But note: no peer-reviewed paper directly measures a "VWAP magnet" touch/reversion rate; that specific claim is unquantified in the literature.

**(c) Criticisms / failure modes:** the published *systematic* evidence favors VWAP as a **trend filter**, not a fade level — the opposite of most retail folklore. Band-fade fails in the exact regime (one-timeframing/trend days) your family-3 stats say happens on ~40–50% of NQ days. Anchored VWAP: **FOLKLORE-ONLY** — Brian Shannon's book/CMT material ([CMT deck](https://cmtassociation.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Shannon-Specific-Anchored-VWAP-Strategies-1.pdf)) contains no backtest; no rigorous published test of anchor-point VWAPs found. Zarattini-family results carry frictionless-fill and regime-dependence caveats (see family 6).

**(d) Data needed:** L1 (typical-price VWAP approximation is standard); L2 makes bands exact and lets you test whether VWAP touches coincide with passive-flow absorption (novel).

## 5. Order flow (delta divergence, absorption, CVD)

**(a) Mechanical definitions:** delta = aggressor buy volume − aggressor sell volume per bar/price (your L2); CVD = running sum; delta divergence = new price high with lower delta/CVD peak (windowed extrema comparison); absorption = volume at a price level ≥ k× baseline with price displacement ≤ m ticks against visible resting liquidity (needs L2 + L3); exhaustion = extreme with collapsing aggressive volume.

**(b) Strongest published quantitative claims — all at the *microstructure mechanism* level [A]:**
- [Cont, Kukanov & Stoikov, "The Price Impact of Order Book Events"](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1712822) (J. Financial Econometrics 12(1), 2014; 50 NYSE stocks, TAQ): short-horizon price changes are driven by **order flow imbalance**, linear impact with slope inversely proportional to depth, robust across stocks/timescales. This is the academic bedrock for "delta moves price."
- [Kolm, Turiel & Westray, "Deep Order Flow Imbalance"](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=3900141) (Mathematical Finance 2023; 115 Nasdaq stocks): order-flow-derived features forecast returns at multiple intraday horizons and beat raw order-book features — flow contains real alpha at short horizons.
- Absorption mechanism: [Frey & Sandås, "The Impact of Iceberg Orders in Limit Order Books"](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1108485) (2009): iceberg orders are 12–20× larger than displayed limit orders; the more of an iceberg executes, the **smaller** its price impact (liquidity, not information), and detected icebergs attract matching market orders. This is published evidence that "high volume without price progress" marks real hidden liquidity — the mechanism behind absorption trades.
- Cautionary tale [A]: Easley/López de Prado/O'Hara's VPIN ("order flow toxicity", [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1695041)) vs [Andersen & Bondarenko's rebuttal](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1881731): VPIN's predictive power vanished once volume/volatility correlation and trade-classification choices were controlled. Directly relevant: delta-derived indicators are exquisitely sensitive to trade-classification and normalization choices.

**(c) The trading-signal grammar is FOLKLORE-ONLY:** no rigorous published win-rate study exists for footprint delta divergence, absorption-reversal, or CVD confirmation as retail-defined. The only numeric claim found — 62% win rate, avg R 2.4 on NQ 2024–25 CVD divergences ([Cofia Trading](https://cofiatrading.com/en/blog/cvd-divergences-guide-complet)) — is an unverifiable internal vendor metric. [QuantifiedStrategies](https://www.quantifiedstrategies.com/order-flow-trading-strategy/) states flatly that essentially no independent order-flow traders run backtested/quantified versions, and that backtested vs realized limit-order fill gaps can flip results. Failure modes to expect: aggressor-classification noise, divergence-window arbitrariness, and absorption being indistinguishable from "big trader done accumulating" vs "big trader about to get run over" without the book context.

**(d) Data needed:** L2 (delta, CVD, footprint) + L3 (resting depth for absorption/iceberg detection). This family is your program's biggest chance to produce genuinely novel published-grade numbers — but with ~270 sessions, define events tightly and report per-event counts.

## 6. Opening Range Breakout (ORB)

**(a) Mechanical definition:** OR = high/low of first n minutes (5/15/30/60); long on break above ORH (short below ORL); stop at opposite OR extreme or ATR-fraction; exits: R-multiple target, VWAP trail, or market-on-close. Lineage: Crabel (1990).

**(b) Strongest published quantitative claims [A — this is the best-evidenced family]:**
- [Holmberg, Lönnbark & Lundström (Finance Research Letters 10(1), 2013)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1544612312000438): US crude futures 30 Mar 1983–26 Jan 2011, ORB returns "significantly higher than zero" with success rates above a fair game — the first peer-reviewed ORB validation.
- [Zarattini & Aziz 2023, "Can Day Trading Really Be Profitable?"](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4416622) (SSRN 4416622): 5-min ORB on QQQ 2016–Feb 2023, direction = first-candle color, stop = first-candle extreme, target 10R or EoD, 1% risk, 4× leverage. From the paper: **1,795 trades (51% long/49% short), annualized net return 31%, Sharpe 1.12, alpha 33% net of commissions**; TQQQ implementation +1,484% total vs +169% QQQ; best variant used stop = 5% of 14-day ATR with no profit target.
- [Zarattini, Barbon & Aziz 2024, "A Profitable Day Trading Strategy for the U.S. Equity Market"](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4729284): 5-min ORB across 7,000+ stocks 2016–2023 filtered to "Stocks in Play" (high relative volume): annualized alpha 33% net, Sharpe 2.4; top-20 Stocks-in-Play portfolio +1,600%+, Sharpe 2.81. Replicated openly on [QuantConnect](https://www.quantconnect.com/research/18444/opening-range-breakout-for-stocks-in-play/).
- Conditional stats on your instrument [B]: [TradingStats ORB research](https://tradingstats.net/orb-strategy-research/) (6,142 ES/NQ days 2014–2026): NQ 30-min ORB double-break 35–46% by weekday, continuation 64–72%; Monday cleanest (lowest double-break), Wednesday choppiest; condition stacks push continuation >80%.

**(c) Criticisms / failure modes / decay [important]:**
- [CXO Advisory's review](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/technical-trading/day-trading-with-an-opening-range-breakout-strategy/) of the QQQ paper: fills assumed at exact 9:35/16:00 prints, **no bid-ask spread, no slippage**, variant selection (TQQQ, ATR-stop) introduces snooping; low-win-rate/10R design is extremely sensitive to execution assumptions.
- **Directly on NQ-family futures [A, negative]:** [Mesfin 2026, "Structural Limits of OHLCV-Based Intraday Signals in MNQ Futures"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004) — 947 days of 5-min MNQ 2021–2025, walk-forward, 2-pt round-trip friction: **every ORB variant failed** (ORB long bar+1: N=447, −0.82 pts net, win 51.9%; ORB long bar+15: +2.82 pts net but T=1.50 and year-unstable (−1.42 in 2022, +7.04 in 2024); ORB pullback entry: 19.3% win, 80.7% stop-out). Across all 14 signal families tested, max gross edge 0.07–1.50 pts vs 2.0 pts friction — a "gross edge ceiling" for bar-level OHLCV signals in micro-Nasdaq. Main failure mode: one strong year masking flat/negative years.
- Regime dependence is confirmed by the friendly replication too: [Quantitativo's ES implementation](https://www.quantitativo.com/p/intraday-momentum-for-es-and-nq) was flat 2010–2017 and only worked 2018+; results degraded materially versus the paper once realistic futures slippage was applied. Holmberg et al.'s crude-oil profits also concentrated in high-volatility periods.
- **Verdict: PUBLISHED-QUANTIFIED — the only family with multiple peer-reviewed/SSRN positives — but with a peer-adjacent negative result on your exact market at retail friction. The edge lives or dies on volatility regime, instrument selection ("in play" filter), and execution costs.**

**(d) Data needed:** L1 only.

## 7. Elliott Wave & Fibonacci retracements (golden pocket)

**(a) Mechanical definitions:** Fib: swing high/low detection (e.g., ZigZag k%), retracement ratios 0.382/0.5/0.618; golden pocket = 0.618–0.65 zone; test = whether reversals cluster at these ratios above chance. Elliott: 5-3 wave grammar — **not mechanically codable as published** (counts are revisable ex post; any coding requires proprietary disambiguation rules).

**(b) Strongest published quantitative work — mostly NEGATIVE [A]:**
- [Batchelor & Ramyar 2006, "Magic Numbers in the Dow"](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/16276/1/magic%20numbers%20in%20the%20dow.pdf) (Cass Business School): turning points in the DJIA 1915–2003; frequencies of price and time ratios between successive trends show **no significant difference from random**; "the idea that prices retrace to a Fibonacci ratio or round fraction of the previous trend clearly lacks any scientific rationale."
- [Automatic identification and evaluation of Fibonacci retracements (Expert Systems with Applications, 2021)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0957417421012495): algorithmic detection across three equity markets — Fibonacci levels have little standalone predictive ability.
- Weak-tier supportive papers exist ([Pakistan cement sector](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/331547753): 27% of supports / 36% of resistances "followed" Fib; [energy stocks/crypto profitability study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8752186/) mixed) — low-power samples, no multiple-testing control.
- Elliott: [D'Angelo & Grimaldi 2017](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/316702779_The_Effectiveness_of_the_Elliott_Waves_Theory_to_Forecast_Financial_Markets_Evidence_from_the_Currency_Market) claims currency-market forecasting effectiveness but with subjective wave identification; critics (Aronson, Mandelbrot) note the theory is unfalsifiable as stated; Prechter's rebuttal to Batchelor–Ramyar conceded no direct counter-evidence. No rigorous positive test of EW as a mechanical system exists.

**(c) Criticisms:** confluence-mining (many ratios × many swings ⇒ something always "hits"), swing-detection dependence, and the golden pocket specifically has zero published support. **Verdict: PUBLISHED-NEGATIVE for Fib predictive clustering; Elliott FOLKLORE-ONLY (untestable as specified). Lowest test priority — useful mainly as a null-control family for your pipeline.**

**(d) Data needed:** L1.

## Cross-cutting takeaways for test prioritization

1. **Priority by evidence density:** ORB/intraday-momentum (family 6) and VWAP-as-trend-filter (family 4) have real published positives *with NQ/ES replications*; range-extension/IB conditionals (3) and single prints/value-touch rates (1) have large-sample tier-B numbers to replicate on your 2023–2026 window; order flow (5) has proven mechanism but an unquantified signal grammar — your L2/L3 layers can produce genuinely new results there; open types (2) and b/P shapes (3) are folklore needing your own frozen definitions; Elliott/Fib (7) is a published-negative control.
2. **The friction ceiling warning** ([Mesfin 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04004)): on MNQ, all fourteen 5-min OHLCV signal families showed gross edges (0.07–1.50 pts) below a 2-pt friction cost, with year-instability the dominant failure mode. Your program should (i) evaluate on full-size NQ economics, (ii) demand cross-year stability, and (iii) expect any surviving edge to come from the layers beyond bar-level OHLCV — exactly your footprint and heatmap data.
3. **Recurring folklore pattern:** every family carries a canonical percentage ("80% rule", "naked POC 80%", "poor highs 78%", "2σ VWAP 75–80%") that traces to vendor copy, not samples — while the actual sampled numbers, where they exist, land lower (VA traverse 27–67%; band-fade 61–63%; single-print fill 64% by D+5) and are heavily regime-conditional.
