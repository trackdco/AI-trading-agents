# Due-diligence report: Thraxx / Christopher Creamer, "Orderflow Trader"

Same method as `research/findings/orochi-credibility.md`. Everything below
is labelled VERIFIED (fetched directly), REPORTED (search snippet only), or
NOT FOUND (absence is the finding).

## 1. IDENTITY

**VERIFIED (fetched):**
- YouTube channel **"Christopher Creamer | Orderflow Trader"**, handle
  `@thraxxtrades`, channel ID `UC_gLIbMvRSx5abw9cjGmaBQ`, **6,450
  subscribers**. Content split: **43 long-form videos, 126 live streams,
  178 shorts**. Latest upload at pull time 2026-07-31.
- **He trades under a full real name.** This is the single largest
  credibility difference from the Orochi intake, which was first-names-only
  and anonymous. A real name is attributable and carries downside.
- **126 live streams**, all titled "🔴 Live NQ Futures Trading | Order Flow
  & Prop Firms", typical duration 3,200–7,700 seconds (roughly 1–2 hours),
  typical viewership 300–1,600. He states repeatedly that he takes trades
  live on stream and calls entries in real time.
- **X/Twitter `@thraxxtrades` exists, joined February 2021** (REPORTED via
  search — the profile itself returned HTTP 402 to our fetcher). If the
  join date is right, the public footprint is **~5.5 years**, versus
  Orochi's ~10 months.

## 2. WHAT HE SELLS, AND WHO PAYS HIM

This is the section that matters for how his content should be read.

**VERIFIED:**
- **Goat Funded Futures affiliate link**, `referral_id=THRAXX`, in video
  descriptions. He earns when a viewer buys a prop-firm evaluation.
- **Prop Firm Match paid sponsorship** — read-aloud sponsor segments in at
  least two teaching videos, with discount code "match". A prop-firm
  comparison/affiliate platform.
- **1-on-1 coaching** sold via a Typeform application link.
- **Gumroad store** (`thraxxtrades.gumroad.com`) — exists and is his;
  product list and prices did not render to our fetcher. **[OPEN]**
- The **"Free Community"** he links (`whop.com/never-flat`) is **not his**.
  It is "Never Flat Trades", **run by SpaghEddie** — 1,600 members, free,
  29 reviews / 4.9 stars — and his link carries a `?ref=` parameter. He is
  referring traffic to another operator's community.

**The structural conflict, stated plainly.** His revenue is tied to viewers
*buying prop-firm evaluations*. Prop firms make money when evaluations
fail. Content that showcases payouts and normalises running many funded
accounts increases evaluation sales. That is a real incentive to present
the profitable side of the ledger, and it applies regardless of whether he
is an honest trader. **This does not make his mechanics wrong — mechanics
are what we test — but it disqualifies every P&L claim on the channel from
being treated as evidence.**

## 3. THE PERFORMANCE CLAIMS

**VERIFIED (that the claims were made, not that they are true):** the
channel posts recurring P&L and payout figures in video titles — January
"+$31,777 | $12,369 in Payouts", February "+$18,909 in Payouts", March
"+$10,848 in Payouts", weekly payout posts of $2,180 / $2,740 / $4,141 /
$4,500, and daily recaps of +$920 to +$8,069.

**NOT FOUND:** no broker statements, no prop-firm payout certificates, no
third-party-verified track record (no Kinfo/MyFxBook-equivalent), no
audited history anywhere reachable.

**The counter-evidence is real and worth weighing.** He also publishes
losses, prominently and by title: "I Blew All My Funded Accounts (-$18,000
in 2 Days)", "I Lost It All on My $500 to $10K Small Account Challenge",
"April Monthly Recap | My Worst Month in a Long Time", "May Monthly Recap |
The Streak is Over (I GOT COOKED)", "Weekly Recap - Worst Week in a Long
Time", "Daily Trading Recap - Broke my rules, BAD day". A pure marketing
operation does not lead with a −$18,000 blowup. In the teaching videos he
describes two years of losing, blowing "two accounts, three accounts, four
accounts in a single session", and calls his earlier trading "a gambling
problem that was basically just dressed up as trading".

**Assessment:** the claims are unverifiable but the *disclosure pattern* is
markedly more honest than the last intake's. Orochi's two recap videos were
post-hoc chart markups with a static P/L overlay and no execution evidence.
Thraxx runs 126 live sessions where trades are called before the outcome is
known. That is a categorically stronger form of evidence — **live calls are
falsifiable in real time** — even though we have not audited any of it.

## 4. FOOTPRINT

**NOT FOUND (absence is the finding):**

  for "thraxx", "thraxxtrades", and "never flat" return **0 hits**.
- No trading-forum threads (futures.io, EliteTrader, ForexFactory), no

**REPORTED:** one search snippet lists him among channels recommended for
learning order-flow basics. Single low-grade mention, not an endorsement of
substance.

## 5. BOTTOM LINE

A named trader with a ~5-year social footprint, 6.5K subscribers, 126 live
NQ order-flow sessions, a documented history of published losses as well as
wins, and **direct financial ties to the prop-firm funnel** (one affiliate
referral, one paid sponsor). No independent reputation exists in either
direction. No verified track record.

**How to use this corpus:** test the mechanics, ignore the money. His
footprint parameters are unusually precise and fully specified (400%
imbalance ratio, 10-contract floor, 300-MNQ big-trade filter, 5-minute
execution), his entry models are genuinely mechanical, and — unlike every
prior intake — **he teaches a stop**. Those are testable objects. Every
dollar figure on the channel is marketing-adjacent and enters no verdict.

**One genuine analytical asset, flagged for the program:** his central
claim — that aggression only means something when measured against whether
price *responded* to it — is the same object our own canon landed on
independently (§5.12-10: flow near-worthless at entry, decisive inside the
trade; edge living in displacement measured against visible liquidity).
Convergence between an outside practitioner and our own measurement is the
best reason in this report to spend a slot here.
