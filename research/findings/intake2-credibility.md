## 1. JadeCap (youtube.com/@JadeCap)

### VERIFIED (fetched directly)

**Identity:** Kyle Ng, "JadeCap" (formerly "JadeCapFx"). Named in Chat With
Traders episode 304 (July 31, 2025): "Kyle Ng aka JadeCap — $4.5M with
Process, Patience, and Prop Firms" — https://chatwithtraders.com/episode/304-kyle-ng
(fetched full page). Background per that page: SUNY Albany, ~14 years trading,
started in forex, transitioned to futures, ICT-influenced.

**Channel (fetched youtube.com/@JadeCap/about):** 230K subscribers, 269
videos, joined Dec 10, 2022. Bio: "World Record Payout Holder. I trade
Markets. I teach Markets. 14+ years in the trenches." Links:
tradingapprentice.com, t.me/jademastersabode ("JadeCap's Trading Room"
Telegram), instagram.com/jadecapofficial. X: @jadecap_.

**What the "world record" actually is:** Largest **single** prop-firm payout —
**$2,552,800.50, one wire, paid by Apex Trader Funding (futures prop),
April 22, 2025**. Verified via fetch of Prop Traders Weekly (Apr 28, 2025):
"For the second week running, ApexTraderFunding has paid out an all-time
industry record. This time $2.55million to Jadecap, beating last week's
record payout of $2.46 million" — quotes his Apr 22, 2025 tweet: "I just
received the BIGGEST single payout in prop firm history from @ApexTradeFund
this morning." https://www.proptraders.io/p/apex-breaks-payout-record-again-pays-trader-2-55million
Also verified via fetch of The Market Journal (Apr 27, 2025): "Fifty-six
sessions, one wire for $2,552,800.50, and more than $4.5 million in lifetime
prop-firm withdrawals... the biggest one-time payout any retail trader has
ever received from a prop firm." https://www.market-journal.com/p/15-key-prop-firm-rules

**Who certifies it:** Nobody independent. **No Guinness record found.** It's a
community/industry-reported record: his own tweet + industry newsletters +
podcast titles; the previous "record" ($2.46M, also Apex, one week earlier,
different trader) shows these are rolling firm-payout records tracked
informally. Apex's own site (apextraderfunding.com/payouts) could not be
fetched (Cloudflare block), so no firm-published certification was directly

**What he sells (fetched):** tradingapprentice.com — a VSL funnel: "The
Trading Apprentice: Get Funded in 90 Days | $4.5M In Proven Prop Firm
Payouts... The Simple Trading System That Made Me $4.5M In Prop Firm Payouts

Funding," plus disclosure "This video may promote products or services I am

**The video in question:** "How To Pass Prop Firm Evaluations (World Record
Holder)" (v2Epolr4olM), premiered May 12, 2026, 84,794 views (fetched watch
page + oembed).

**"Daily Sweep" as described externally (fetched):** Third-party write-up at
https://time-price-research-astrofin.blogspot.com/2026/02/one-trading-strategy-for-life-daily.html

quotes him crediting the strategy with "a world-record $2.5 million payout

Strategy I Will Use for Life" (dcIcXbzMcc4) and "The 'Daily Sweep' Is The
EASIEST Way To Become Profitable FAST" (nuYlnDwgEgg).

**Instruments/sessions:** NY session (8:00 a.m. prep, 9:30 ET open) index
futures at Apex; ICT-derived (he has a video "All Five ICT Entry Models
Explained"). Chat With Traders tags the episode "forex, futures" — forex
background, futures now.

## 2. MrZincx (youtube.com/@MrZincx)

### VERIFIED (fetched directly)

**Channel (fetched /about + /videos):** "Mr Zinc," 5.21K subscribers, 143
videos, joined Mar 27, 2024. Channel description still reads "Selling options
to retirement" — the channel visibly pivoted from options-selling content to
futures (video list goes from "Options Selling With Zinc" era to
IB/London-50/prop content). Links: twitter.com/MrZincx, free Discord.
Real name: not published anywhere found.

**What he sells:** Nothing directly, based on all fetched descriptions — free
Discord; monetization is **affiliate links**: Edgeful ("Try Edgeful 7 Days
Free Here: edgeful.com/?via=zinc") and Lucid ("Use code 'zinc' with Lucid to
get 40% off your first 5 accounts!") — both verified in fetched video
descriptions.

**Lucid relationship (fetched):** "Lucid Trading Review, Everything You Need
To Know" (msDrciMScmE, Mar 28, 2026): "Lucid Trading is, in my opinion, the
best firm in the space right now" + the affiliate code above. He also
publicly defends Lucid on X against critics.

**Edgeful dependence — confirmed, and openly credited.** This is not hidden
derivation; he names Edgeful as his data source and affiliates for them:
- "Data Backed Trading | Edgeful" (lMy_d9CC6tM, Oct 9, 2025): "Data is the
  fastest way to remove emotions when trading..." + payout claim "I just took
  a $22,500 payout from TopStep."
- "Learning The IB By Rejection & London 50 Model" (Gze5nSpTzGw, Jan 1,
  2026): "For the data provider Edgeful... This [product] has tremendously
  helped my trading performance."
- **"IB by rejection" is literally the name of an Edgeful subreport.**
  Verified by fetching edgeful.com/blog/posts/initial-balance-breakout-report
  (Apr 15, 2026): the IB breakout report has 10 subreports, including "**by
  rejection**: analyzes which initial balance boundary (high or low) forms
  first during the IB period, then tracks which boundary gets broken first
  after the IB period ends."
- "London 50" is his own adaptation of another Edgeful stat. His words,
  fetched from video g391-mfnRzE (Nov 5, 2025): "The London 50 is my own
  personal trading model. I tweaked the previous days 50 into using it for
  the London Session."

**Edgeful's published numbers (fetched from edgeful.com blog):**
- NQ initial balance (IB = 9:30-10:30 ET first hour), last 6 months, from
  edgeful.com/blog/posts/ultimate-bullish-setup-trading-NQ: **single break
  82%, double break 16%, no break ~1.55%**; IB 25% retracement level hit 65%
  of the time (entry level), 50% retrace hit only ~26.67% (stop level);
  first-hour-green → day-closes-green 84%; open-inside-prior-range → tags
  prior high/low ~75%.
- London-session-related, from
  edgeful.com/blog/posts/market-session-breakout-report-trading-strategy
  (YM, last 6 months): **NY breaks one side of London's range 83.2% (single
  break), both sides 4.8%, stays inside London range 12%**; YM IB: 76/20/4.

### REPORTED (snippets only)

### NOT FOUND

- Any paid course/community/mentorship.

in 30 minutes. One setup. One timeframe. No complexity." Only external link:
instagram.com/daxtontrades. Identity: first name "Daxton" only (payout emails
say "Hello Daxton"); no surname or location published.

**Claims (video titles, fetched):** Escalating P&L series — "$191K", "$207K"
("I Quit ICT and Made $207K"), "$228K in 120 days", "$242K", "$257K" ("I
Turned FVGs Into $257K"), "**I made $300K with MNQ taking only 1 Trade/Day

"**Backtesting My 72% Win Rate NQ Setup (1 Trade/day)**" (8G4QAFjk7WA). The
$300K video description (fetched) describes the setup: bias via a custom EMA
indicator, counter-trend identification, **inverted fair value gap** entry,
one timeframe (5-min per his own tags), 1 trade/day, MNQ/NQ.

**What he sells (fetched daxtontrades.com + apply.html):** 1-on-1 mentorship,
application-funnel model: "The Only Mentorship That GUARANTEES You'll Finally
Become 6-Figures Funded And Profitable OR YOU GET A $1,000 REFUND... Only 5
Spots Left — CAP ON 5." Application asks how much you can invest: tiers
"$4,000+ USD," "$3,000-$4,000," "Under $3,000 (Not ready to invest yet)" —
i.e., a **$3-4K+ mentorship**. Site claims "VERIFIED RESULTS $281,000+ IN
2026 SO FAR."

**Payout proofs (downloaded the actual images from his site):** 10
self-hosted screenshots at daxtontrades.com/images/. Inspected three:
- payout1.jpeg: Wise email — "Hello Daxton, You received **4,058.25 USD from
  TopstepTrader LLC**."
- payout5.png: Rise email — "Hi Daxton, You just received **$3991 from Alpha
  Capital Group Limited**."
- payout9.png: **Lucid Trading payout certificate — $9,000, March 6, 2026**,
  funded account, signed "Campanella, CEO, Founder."
So his props are **Topstep, Alpha Capital Group (Alpha Futures), and Lucid**.
Note the scale mismatch: the visible proofs are $3-9K payouts, while the
marketing claims $281K-$300K; nothing close to $300K is evidenced in the
proofs inspected, and none are third-party verifiable.

**FCA Warning (fetched fca.org.uk/news/warnings/daxtontrades):** The UK
Financial Conduct Authority published an unauthorised-firm warning for
"**@Daxtontrades**" — first published **14/10/2024** — listing "Instagram:
daxtontrades, Facebook: daxtontrades, Telegram: t.me/DaxtonTrades", stating
the firm "may be providing or promoting financial services or products
without our permission. You should avoid dealing with this firm and beware of
scams." Important nuances, stated factually: (a) the Instagram handle in the
FCA warning is **exactly the handle his YouTube and website link to**; (b)
the warning predates his YouTube channel by ~9 months; (c) FCA warnings
sometimes target impersonator/clone accounts, and the FCA itself notes such
firms "may give you details that belong to another business or individual" —
so this is either the same operator pre-YouTube or someone using his handle;
not determinable from available evidence. BrokerChooser echoes the FCA entry

## Cross-cutting observations (factual)
- All three monetize the audience, differently: JadeCap = high-production
  funnel course (tradingapprentice.com) + Apex affiliate code; MrZincx =
  free Discord + Edgeful/Lucid affiliate links; Daxton = $3-4K
  application-gated 1-on-1 mentorship with scarcity framing ("5 spots") +
  refund guarantee.
- Only JadeCap's headline number is externally corroborated (industry
  newsletters + the paying firm's public celebration of it), and even that
  is a firm-reported record with no independent certifier and an eventual
  affiliate relationship with the same firm. Daxton's numbers are entirely
  self-published; the FCA warning on his exact handle is the single external
  data point on him. MrZincx's numbers are small, plausible, self-published,
  and conflicted on Lucid via affiliate code.
