# Sweep record — NY pre-market NQ-native stats angle (agent sweep, 2026-08-04)

Sourcing: WebSearch extracts; direct fetches 403-blocked. URLs = deep-dive leads.

## Concepts (8)

**T1 on-range-single-side-break** — [measured-community, 2,827 days] 94.2% of
days break ≥1 ON level; only one side 75.7%; open-vs-ON-midpoint predicts which
side first at ~76% (+aligned gap 82–84%). Median first break 09:41; 33.5% in
first 5 min; 68.7% by 10:00. Enter toward predicted extreme on first pullback
holding the opening 5-min range; dead by 10:30.

**T2 aln-partial-engulf** — [measured-community, STRONG: nqstats 2,542 sessions
+ Herman 4,262 days independent] classified at the 08:00 ET close: London broke
Asia high & held Asia low ("partial engulf up", 41% of sessions) → NY breaks the
pattern high 80.8%; engulf-down → low breaks 75%. Edge degrades ~30pp if the
wrong side breaks first (built-in invalidation). This IS the Europe→US handoff,
measured — buy 08:00–09:30 pullbacks in engulf direction. Wrong side: US
pre-market faders calling a Europe trend "extended."

**T3 overnight-extension-fade** — [academic + practitioner convergence] Liu-Tse:
overnight return NEGATIVELY predicts the first half-hour (and positively the
last — Gao's channel); Lou-Polk-Skouras tug-of-war; = Dalton's inventory
correction. Fade the open toward premarket VWAP/settlement when inventory
~100% one-sided AND open at ON extreme AND gap not news-made. Re-verify
post-2021 (regime changed).

**T4 magic-hours-pre830** — [measured-community] NQ hourly-range false-break
reversion best at 06:00/07:00/08:00 ET (76–83.5%, 1,287 days); structurally
drift-free 04:00–08:30 stretch (Bondarenko-Muravyev: the ~20 non-Euro-open hours
average zero). POPULARIZED 2025 — pre/post split mandatory; slippage is the real
threat (thin book), depth data prices it.

**T5 0830-impulse-open-continuation** — [academic mechanism + unpublished
bridge] ABDV: macro surprises = 1-min jumps, post-release price efficient; Gao:
momentum stronger on news days. The unpublished NQ question: does the
08:30–09:00 impulse direction predict the 09:30 open-drive/first IB-break side?
Wrong-side pre-release positioners liquidating into the open ARE the drive.

**T6 premarket-range-vol-forecast** — SPEC LAYER. [academic + community] E-mini
vol measured 07:45–09:30 is the best pre-market predictor of 09:30–11:30 vol
(Hawkes study); narrow-IB days extend ≥1×IB 41% vs 15.5% wide (1,586 sessions).
Premarket range percentile at 09:15 switches breakout-vs-fade logic and sizing.

**T7 opening-sweep-reverse** — [folklore + partial measurement] "Judas at
09:30": first-minutes break of a PM extreme that closes back inside = stop
harvest, fade to premarket VWAP/ON mid. The unpublished conditional worth
extracting: sweep probability when the NON-predicted extreme (per T1's
open-location filter) breaks first. Most crowded retail narrative 2023–26 —
crowded stops make the mechanics real; naive FVG entries surely arbed. CVD
absorption at the extreme is load-bearing.

**T8 gap-survival-switch** — [measured-community] 34% of NQ gap fills in first
5 min; only 39% of fills occur after the first half hour; 86% within 2.5h → a
gap surviving to 10:00 unfilled flips to continuation. Fade small no-news gaps
with 10:00 time-stop; flip to gap-direction on survival.
