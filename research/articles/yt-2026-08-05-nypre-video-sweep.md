## What's usable

**U1 — Retail is taught to *mark* the pre-market, not to *trade* it.** Every
routine video in this corpus does the same thing: draw pre-market high, pre-market
low, previous-day high/low before 09:30, then wait for the open. Chloe Trades
[QYDf4oIpUtY @ 7:00]: *"you have previous day high level... then you have pre-market
high right there and then I use a dotted line and then you have pre-market low."*
Chad Trades [-UBuD0rEumc @ 2:40] and Carmine Rosato [c7MX1OjPC90 @ 2:29] describe
the same ritual. **The tradeable inference is not the setup — it is that PMH/PML are
a known, dense, pre-committed order location by 09:30.** Whoever breaks them has a
counterparty whose orders were placed hours earlier and are not being managed.

**U2 — The one mechanical pre-market-level setup found, and it fires after the open.**
Trade Your Edge [azoKoV61lMU @ 3:32-4:19]: *"as price drops toward this pre-market
low, this is the first power bar... large-bodied candle that forms at or near this
pre-market level. That's your buy trigger... don't enter until after this power bar
which formed near the pre-market low is complete."* Note the geometry: entry above a
completed impulse candle, stop under its low — a naturally tight stop, which is the
property the euro-handoff tombstone says our failures lacked.

**U3 — The thin book is the pre-market's defining property, and it is what makes
pre-market breaks unreliable.** NinjaTrader states the mechanism cleanly: when there
is low volume in pre-market, NQ breaks a resistance level, enticing longs; the
regular session then opens with much higher liquidity and price drops back below,
trapping the breakout buyers, whose exits fuel the reversal. **The adjudicating event
is the arrival of real liquidity at 09:30 — not the passage of time.**

**U4 — Retail is structurally under-represented pre-market, for a boring reason.**
J.D. Hyter [X_FYfGGAjeg @ 1:19-1:34]: *"the margin requirement in the intraday time
between 9:30 and 4 is $500 to trade one contract, well the overnight you need
$116,000 to trade one contract, so just by default most retail traders are excluded
from trading any other time."* Day-trade margin is an RTH concession; outside it the
full maintenance margin applies. This is a checkable claim about *who is in the book*
before 09:30 and it argues the pre-market counterparty mix is institutional/algo.

**U5 — The open is when accumulated orders execute, not when new information starts.**
Trade Your Edge [F9_uLdSYOrc @ 0:58]: *"what's actually happening in those first few
minutes is the market is sorting itself out. Overnight and early morning orders
coming into trading desks are getting filled. News is getting priced in."* Same
inference as U3 from the opposite direction: 09:30 is a scheduled liquidity event.

**U6 — Practitioners treat 08:30 as a hazard to be flat for, not an edge.**
TTrades [hKCMqh0ZsY0 @ 2:19]: *"I will just avoid prior to 8:30 on Tuesday... with
PPI since that is under a medium impact I'm just going to avoid prior to 8:30."*
Widespread pre-print flattening is itself a one-sided, deadline-driven flow — which
is the mechanism behind the de-risking drift, from the participants' own mouths.

## What's noise

**N1 — "Pre-market plan" content is process advice, not an edge.** The bulk of the
corpus (-UBuD0rEumc, QYDf4oIpUtY, and the FuturestraderElite daily series filtered
out at <3k views) is *"mark your levels, know your bias."* No stated trigger, no
stated invalidation, no measured base rate. Nothing to census.

**N2 — Stock pre-market does not transfer.** Ross Cameron's 57-minute pre-market
video [PjVivCcM1B0] is the corpus's most-viewed pre-market-specific source and is
about small-cap gap-and-go: order routing, ECN limitations, halts. NQ has none of
those constraints. Filed as read; not applicable.

**N3 — Retail conflates "pre-market" with "the London session."** Jdub Trades
[lYThcYGUw7I @ 5:18]: *"We have the pre-market which is also called the London
session."* These are different clocks with different participants. Worth knowing
because it means "pre-market" search results are polluted with London-session
content, and because our own London lane must not be assumed to share findings.

**N4 — ORB content is about 09:30+, and its own backtesters do not find an edge in
it.** TOS Indicators [MOG-DbgmzzI], titled on 1,178,668 backtested ORB trades,
resolves into a subscription pitch rather than published conditionals. Scarface
Trades [FOVhnNiP8AI] scored highest on rule density in the whole corpus and is
entirely post-open. Excluded from ny-pre on clock grounds.

**N5 — Liquidity-sweep / SMC content asserts base rates without sourcing them.**
The "pattern fails 25-35% of the time" figure circulating in the sweep literature
carries no sample, no instrument and no window. Treated as folklore. The *mechanism*
(stops cluster at watched levels) is retained; the *numbers* are not.

## Contradictions between sources

**C1 — Is the pre-market tradeable at all?** The retail consensus is no: trade the
levels after 09:30. HolaPrime states the pre-market has wider spreads and slower
fills and is "generally less favorable." Against that, the academic record documents
economically large, statistically significant returns concentrated in specific
pre-open windows (see `2026-08-05-nypre-inventory-academic.md`). **The disagreement
is resolvable and it is the crux: retail avoids the window for execution-cost
reasons, which is precisely the condition under which an edge survives being
published.** Our cost stack, not opinion, adjudicates this — and it is why the 2x
slippage arm matters more here than in any RTH candidate.

**C2 — Does a pre-market break mean continuation or trap?** NinjaTrader says a
thin-book break traps and reverses at the open. The overnight-continuation
literature (edgeful) says pre-market direction carries into the open. Both cannot be
unconditional. **The conditioning variable is whichever separates them — most likely
whether the break was backed by real resting size or by a vacuum.** We hold MBP-10
depth for this window, which is the instrument that answers it. This contradiction
is the seed of candidate `nypre-thin-break-trap`.

**C3 — 08:30 as hazard or opportunity.** Practitioners flatten before the print
(U6). The prior sweep's `nypre-prerelease-premium` claimed a positive risk premium
for holding into it. Our own census measured the opposite sign in both eras. Three
positions, and the two evidenced ones agree against the published one.

## Candidate leads

- `nypre-thin-break-trap` — NEW, from U1 + U3 + C2. The pre-market break adjudicated
  by the arrival of liquidity at 09:30, not by time. Explicitly distinct from the
  killed `nypre-quiet-hours-reversion`, which required reversion to an hourly mid
  *within the same hour* and therefore had no forcing event.
- `nypre-closing-imbalance-unwind` — from the academic record, not this corpus. See
  `2026-08-05-nypre-inventory-academic.md`.
- `nypre-derisk-into-print` — U6 supplies the participant-side testimony for the
  sign our own `nypre-prerelease-premium` census already measured.

## Method note

First sweep in this repo built on fetched transcripts rather than search snippets
(`research/README.md` § Video sources). 12 queries → 187 unique videos → 78 passing
a >=8 min / >=3k view filter → 21 transcribed, 536,150 characters, all committed
under `research/youtube/transcripts/`. Every quote above carries `[video_id @ mm:ss]`
and is checkable against the cached file. Three fetches were blocked by YouTube
rate-limiting and are listed above rather than silently dropped.

**Honest summary of yield: this corpus is thin.** Mainstream video has very little
genuine NQ pre-market mechanism in it — the session is under-covered relative to the
09:30 open. That absence is itself the most useful thing the sweep found (C1), but
nobody should read this file expecting a strategy to fall out of it. The strategy
material in this round came from the academic sources.
