# Sweep record — NY pre-market macro-announcement angle (agent sweep, 2026-08-04)

Sourcing: WebSearch extracts; direct fetches 403-blocked. URLs = deep-dive leads.

## Evidence state (read first)

- Announcement-day risk premium real (Savor-Wilson ~11.4bp vs 1.1bp; Ernst et al.
  correct it down but ~half the equity premium is announcement-day).
- Pre-announcement drift, two channels: informed-leakage (Kurov, ~30min before —
  KILLED by 2018 lockup reforms, "Drift Begone!" 2022) and risk-premium
  (Hu-Pan-Wang-Zhu JFE 2021: 7–10bp/event overnight before NFP/ISM/GDP,
  conditional on elevated uncertainty — SURVIVES conditionally).
- Post-release: price adjustment ~complete in 1 min (Ederington-Lee), vol
  elevated ~15 min, first-minute overshoot corrected minutes 2–3.
- Naive post-release continuation FALSIFIED on MNQ (arXiv 2605.04004): all
  "drift" inside bars 1–5; bar+6 unconditional t = 0.14–0.69. Conditional
  (magnitude-gated, pullback-entry) forms untested there.
- Pre-FOMC drift (Lucca-Moench): dead post-2015 unconditionally.
- Surprise proxy: first-minute reaction (no consensus feed needed) — and per
  Boyd-Hu-Jagannathan the reaction is the RIGHT object anyway (equity response
  to the same surprise flips sign by regime).
- CPI vol regime: 2022–23 monster CPI days are gone (straddles collapsed by
  late 2024) — regime split mandatory.

## Concepts (8)

**M1 release-tier-regime-gate** — SPEC LAYER. Tag days {tier-1 8:30, tier-2,
9:15, 10:00, FOMC, empty}; split every system's P&L by tag; suppress pre-08:30
entries / force-flat 08:28 on tier-1. The incumbent canon almost certainly has
different pre-leg P&L by tier and doesn't know it — cheap audit, immediate value.

**M2 threshold-gated-reaction-continuation** — first-minute reaction ≥ k× baseline
(true surprise) → queued same-direction flow (cash accounts, open-benchmarked
funds) must still transact → enter impulse direction on the first <50% pullback
after 08:35. The falsification paper killed the UNCONDITIONAL form; the gate +
pullback entry is where survivors live. CVD absorption filter is the strongest
upgrade. Crowding: HFT owns the seconds; minutes-to-hour on 08:30 NQ much less.

**M3 spike-reject-fade** — impulse into a deliberately thinned book overshoots;
if a 1-min close returns INSIDE the 08:00–08:29 pre-release range within M min,
the move was liquidity not information — fade toward pre-release mid. M2/M3 are
ONE two-branch event tree (hold vs fail). Objective trigger waits for the trapped
crowd to exist, feeds partly on early discretionary faders.

**M4 uncertainty-conditioned-pre-release-drift** — HPWZ risk-premium channel:
long 04:00→08:25 flat before the print, ONLY when trailing vol is top-tercile
and only NFP/GDP-class days. Small, boring, conditional — a tilt, not a system.

**M5 post-data-hold-into-open** — reaction held to 09:15 (price beyond 50% of
post-print move) → opening flow extends it (news-day momentum, Gao); enter
09:15–09:25 BEFORE the ORB crowd, exit minutes after the open. Flags: holds
through 09:30 (execution-semantics ruling needed); 10:00 release days force
early exit.

**M6 news-minted-gap-discrimination** — decompose the opening gap at 09:25 into
pre-08:30 vs post-08:30 components: flow gaps (empty mornings) fade; news-minted
held gaps do NOT (cousin of outside-range 8% fill rate); print-reversed-the-gap
days trap the overnight crowd. Absent from every public gap study. Value shows
up largely as avoided losses.

**M7 fomc-morning-compression** — SPEC LAYER + small fade. FOMC 14:00 makes the
morning structurally fuel-less (vol/volume 20–35% below average): suppress/halve
the canon's pre entries (audit its FOMC-day expectancy FIRST — may pay for the
whole research), optionally fade ON-range extensions toward ON VWAP. Regime
fact, not an anomaly — doesn't decay.

**M8 minor-release-attention-gate** — claims (every Thursday!)/PPI with a HIGHER
magnitude gate: low-attention shocks digest slowly (fewer eyes, thinner instant
arb). Same machinery as M2, tier-2 calendar, ~6–15 triggers/yr. Risk is sample
size and gate-loosening temptation, not crowding.

## Cross-cutting
