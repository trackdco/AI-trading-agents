## AMT#1 london-overnight-inventory-fade → candidate `london-inventory-fade`
The institutional version of the inventory story: NY Fed staff research ("The
Overnight Drift", Boyarchenko/Larsen/Whelan) documents the historical US equity
premium concentrating in the 02:00–03:00 ET hour at the European open, driven by
prior-close order imbalance — dealers unwinding inventory at the first deep pool.
CRITICAL CAVEAT: the Fed's own follow-up ("The Disappearing Overnight Drift")
shows the naive long-at-2am form decayed to ~zero post-2021. The conditional
(skew-triggered, either-direction) correction is the tradeable residue.
Sources: newyorkfed.org/research/staff_reports/sr917 ·
libertystreeteconomics.newyorkfed.org/2026/07/the-disappearing-overnight-drift/ ·
shadowtrader.net/glossary/overnight-inventory/ · marketcalls.in/market-profile/...
Crowding: academic form published + arbed; conditional London NQ form uncrowded.

## AMT#2 asia-range-sweep-reversal → candidate `london-asia-sweep-reversal`
Dalton's open-test-drive at session scale: probe beyond the Asian balance finds no
new business (auction failure), traps the breakout crowd, drives through the
opposite extreme. FX literature: straight London-breakout entries run 40–60% WR
with heavy false-break attrition — demand the failure confirmation.

## AMT#3 london-ib-extension → candidate `london-euro-open-drive`
The 03:00–04:00 ET hour is a legitimate London initial balance. IB built entirely
outside Asian value + one-time-framing = other-timeframe conviction; break extends
the repricing. IB stats (ES/NQ RTH: ~97% of IBs break; small IBs extend furthest)
port as concept, not numbers. Axia (London prop educator) teaches this on FDAX/FTSE.

## AMT#4 poor-extreme-revisit → candidate `london-level-trap-fade` (level-quality conditioning)
Poor/flat prior-RTH extremes (multiple equal touches, no excess) = incomplete
auction + unfinished business. Two-stage playbook: first revisit tends to HOLD
(fade it); subsequent revisit tends to BREAK-AND-RUN (go with it). London is often
the session that travels back to these references. Orthodox view that overnight
doesn't "repair" RTH structure actually helps — the level keeps its status.
Sources: axiafutures.com/blog/trading-strategies-around-poor-lows-and-poor-highs/ ·
marketcalls.in/market-profile/poor-high-and-poor-low-... · shadowtrader.net/glossary/poor-high/
Crowding: known inside Dalton/Axia school, obscure outside; uncrowded mechanized.

## AMT#5 naked-poc-magnet → candidate `london-value-traverse`
Untested prior POCs attract price (~80% revisited within 10 sessions, commonly
cited). Absent fresh news, London's job is repricing against existing references —
the nearest naked POC is the rotation's destination. Go-with the traverse, scale
out into the touch; optional responsive fade of the first touch.

## AMT#6 london-80pct-value-traverse → candidate `london-value-traverse`
Dalton's 80% rule re-anchored: Asia holds outside prior US RTH value all night;
London drives back inside and HOLDS = overnight inventory judged mispriced,
rotation through the full value area with overnight stop-outs as fuel. Published
ES testing: true traverse rate nearer 60% than 80% — bias, not certainty. Full
traverse often completes only in NY — scale/runner by rule.

## AMT#7 london-open-type-gate → SPEC LAYER (open-type gate)
Dalton opening types applied to 03:00 ET as a synthetic open: open-drive (never
returns through the open) = decided institutional business, don't fade; open-test-
drive = best trade location of any open; open-auction inside overnight value = no
conviction. Doubles as the regime gate for every fade-type candidate.

## AMT#8 lvn-traverse-to-hvn → candidate `london-value-traverse` (path mechanism)
Composite-profile LVNs are air pockets: London pushes out of balance into an LVN
and traverses fast to the next HVN — no established business to slow it. European
desks reprice the composite while the map is still clean. Main risk is definitional
fragility (node detection), not crowding.
