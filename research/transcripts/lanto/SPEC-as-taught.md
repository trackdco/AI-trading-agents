## 1. Named setup: IFVG + FVG ["The Setup Behind My Best Results", HIS-WORDS]

The two primitives, family-standard definitions [FAMILY]:

- **FVG (fair value gap):** three-candle displacement imbalance — for a
  bullish gap, `high(i−1) < low(i+1)`; the zone is
  `[high(i−1), low(i+1)]`, created by displacement candle `i`. Traded
  WITH the creating move on a retrace into the zone (continuation).
- **IFVG (inversion fair value gap):** an FVG that FAILS — price closes
  through the far side of the gap. The zone flips polarity: a failed
  bullish gap becomes resistance to sell against, a failed bearish gap
  becomes support to buy against (reversal/shift signal).

His two named entry-quality requirements [HIS-WORDS, from site copy]:

1. **Displacement** — the move creating/inverting the gap must be
   energetic (his site names "displacement" as the entry technique).
2. **Strong candle close** — confirmation is a candle CLOSE, not a
   touch (site: "displacement and strong candle close").

Assembled model, to be confirmed [FAMILY + HIS-WORDS]:

> Displacement leg creates an FVG → the gap is inverted by a strong
> candle close through it (IFVG) → entry on the retest of the inverted
> zone in the new direction, stop beyond the displacement/sweep extreme,
> target at opposing liquidity or the next unmitigated gap. The "+ FVG"
> in his title likely means a same-direction FVG created by the
> inversion leg serves as the entry zone when the IFVG itself is not
> retested cleanly — [GAP], confirm.
