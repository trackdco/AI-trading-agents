## External reconstruction — **UNVERIFIED, NOT HIS STATED RULE**

Per the grounding rules, generic ICT theory must not be laundered into his card. The
following is standard ICT-canon usage, recorded **separately** and flagged, offered to Brake
for confirmation or correction. **It must not be promoted into the card unless he confirms
it matches what the trader actually does.**

- **Order block (canon):** the last opposing candle before a displacement move that breaks
  structure — for a bullish OB, the final down-candle before the up-move. Usually the body,
  sometimes the full range.
- **Inverse order block:** an OB that price traded through, flipping polarity — a failed
  bullish OB becomes resistance. *This is consistent with his usage but he never says it.*
- **Breaker vs mitigation:** breaker = the OB's swing took out prior liquidity before
  structure broke back; mitigation = it did not. ***His stated rule matches the canon here
  exactly*** `[pD5l_gEje9I @ 00:42]` — the one place external theory and his words align.
- **Draw on liquidity (canon):** the nearest significant pool of resting stops — old
  highs/lows, session extremes — that price is likely to seek. *"Clear where price is drawing
  to"* would then mean an obvious unswept pool in one direction.
- **Bias aggregation:** the canon has **no single rule**. Common practice is HTF sets
  directional bias, LTF times entry — consistent with his one example, but this is the
  weakest of the four and I would not rely on it.
