VIDEO 1 — "The Only Orderflow Guide You'll Ever Need" (Pz8f0wWW12M)

OVERALL MARKET MODEL
- Market = interaction of aggressive (market orders) and passive (limit orders) forces routed through a central exchange, represented as volume [01:00-01:30]. Aggressive orders "create pressure" and move price; passive orders "create resistance" [09:50-10:20]. Price moves up/down when market orders consume (sweep) resting limit levels; if best bid/ask is insufficient, price jumps levels with slippage [03:35-05:08].
- Passive orders have price certainty but execution uncertainty; market orders have execution certainty but price uncertainty [05:08-06:10].
- "Path of least resistance": the side of the book with fewer total resting contracts is easier to pierce; e.g. 122 contracts one side vs 273 the other — the 122 side breaks easier [10:20-11:22]. Framed explicitly as probability, not certainty [11:22].
- Auction Market Theory layer: volume profile shows where institutions "build value." Value area = 68% of volume; POC = maximum-transaction level = "strong price acceptance," balanced market [19:40-21:43]. Trends start when price+volume accept OUTSIDE the value area ("out of balance… accumulation or distribution is finished") [21:43-22:14]. Low volume nodes (LVN) = inefficient delivery, poor fills, no price acceptance — market "likes to rebalance the low volume node before continuing in the direction of the trend" [22:14-23:46].
- Hierarchy stated: (1) qualify areas with volume/profile ("it can be volume profile, it can be VWAP, it can be… supply and demand"), (2) read execution of big participants at that area, (3) manage on result/price [39:08-40:40]. "This is the institutional reference on how to analyze the bias using volume, so actual executed orders, not theory" [28:22].

ORDERFLOW PATTERN VOCABULARY (used as triggers everywhere else)
- ABSORPTION: big aggressive effort with zero price reward; "no reward for buyers and… a lot of buy pressure. Probably this candle will have positive delta but negative closure. This is the textbook example for absorption" [11:54-12:25]. Also mid-candle absorption where a big aggressive strike is eaten at one horizontal level and candle closes opposite [17:06-17:37].
- AGGRESSION / INITIATIVE AUCTION: big effort AND big result — "big buy effort got rewarded… initiative auction… higher probability to see a continuation on the next candles" [12:25-13:27]; one-side prints ("imbalance") + strong delta + candle closing in that direction = "really strong point to join the trend" [15:34-16:35].
- BOOK SWEEPING: low effort, high result — few passive players in an area, small aggression sweeps multiple book levels [13:27-13:59].
- EXHAUSTION: decreasing volume into a push + contrarian imbalance at the extreme; price snaps back to value — "amazing opportunities to capitalize on a reversal trade"; vertical version = "dry up in volume… usually… a sudden reversal in price" [18:08-19:10].
- DELTA: difference between aggressive buys and sells per level (delta footprint) or per candle (vertical delta) [13:59-15:34].
