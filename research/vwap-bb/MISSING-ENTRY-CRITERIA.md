## Why this is the largest open item on the project

**The hand log was produced by a process that includes these filters. The spec does not have
them.** The 68.4% in-scope win rate — the only direct evidence this strategy works — was
generated by a trader who was also refusing setups for unfilled range and absent liquidity
sweeps. The frozen spec will take those trades.

**They are therefore not the same strategy**, and the hand log is weaker evidence for the spec
than its headline number suggests. `PREREGISTRATION.md` §8.2 already says the spec "trades faster
and smaller than the hand log did"; this is the same problem one level deeper — it also **trades
setups the author rejects.**

**The gate that existed to catch exactly this is gone.** §12.2's calibration — *"February 2026
re-run must approximately reproduce the 28 hand trades; divergences audited one by one"* — is
irrecoverable, because February 2026 has no bars and the MBP-10 schema cannot produce the
detector's inputs (A3, A6). A trade-by-trade audit would have surfaced these two filters on the
first divergence. **Instead they surfaced by accident, from one question about one chart.**

**And it reopens gate 4.** Gate 4 (SPECIFIABILITY) asks whether there is a stated rule for every
trade decision. It was closed by A7 on the strength of the *selector* being written down. There
are now two known **skip** criteria with no stated rule. Whether this changes the sign of
expectancy — gate 4's termination test — **cannot be known without reading the sealed result**,
so gate 4 cannot be re-closed by argument.

## Both are computable, and that matters

Neither is exotic, and neither depends on the excluded MIG tool:

| criterion | how it could be specified | inputs |
|---|---|---|
| **Unfilled range** | A three-candle imbalance (candle *n−1* high below candle *n+1* low, or the mirror) that price has not since traded back through. Or, more directly matching what Angus described: the fraction of a fast move's range that has been retraced, with a threshold | **1-minute bars. Already held** |
| **Liquidity sweep** | Price trades through a prior confirmed swing extreme and closes back on the origin side within *k* bars. The spec already defines fractal swings (15m, N=2, gate 4 #3) | **1-minute bars. Already held** |

**This is a fixable gap, not an irrecoverable one.** It needs Angus to state the rules, and it
needs an amendment.

## What this does NOT establish

Stated so the finding is not read as broader than it is.

- **One session, one setup, one conversation.** Two criteria surfaced. There may be more, and
  the same method — put his judgement next to the detector's on a chart — is the only way found
  so far that surfaces them.
- **No claim that adding them improves anything.** They would reduce trade frequency, and the
  frequency budget has room (2.84/session against a 2.01 tripwire at the harshest correction).
  Whether they improve expectancy is unknown and unknowable without spending the holdout.
- **The 09:41 rejection was already correct.** §7 invalidation blocked it, and Angus would also
  have skipped it. **The right answer for the wrong reason** — the filter that fired was the 1σ
  extension rule, not either of the criteria he actually applied.
