# Gap fill — the FAR edge of an FVG, not the 50%

## What it covers
The complement to his usual 50%-of-FVG level: **the far edge of the gap**, i.e. the fullest
possible fill.

> *"the very low of a bullish fair value gap… the most discount level of a bullish fair value
> gap is going to be super sensitive. It's going to fill this entire gap."* `[86DOt135Wts @ 00:50]`

He is explicit that this is a **trade-off against the 50%**, not a replacement:
> *"if you only use the very low of the fair value gaps like I just showed, you're also going
> to miss a fair amount of entries… do you want the best entry or do you want to actually be
> in more trades"* `[@ 02:50]`

## The stated mechanism
The gap is an inefficiency; filling all of it *"often times is going to cause a reaction"*
`[@ 01:15]`. Any timeframe works; lower timeframes just give a better entry `[@ 02:26]`.

## The confluence he insists on
> *"combine this with your other levels and probably most importantly combine it with
> liquidity. Engineered liquidity is super powerful."* `[@ 03:35]`
