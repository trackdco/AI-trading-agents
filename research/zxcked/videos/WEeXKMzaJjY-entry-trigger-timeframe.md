# Entry-trigger timeframe — 1-minute vs 5-minute, argued with a full day of examples

## The thesis
> *"Stop obsessing over the one minute time frame."* `[WEeXKMzaJjY @ 03:10]`
He then walks **every one-minute entry trigger** on an NFP day, showing most were stopped, then
shows the 5-minute trigger catching the low of day.

**This is the single most testable claim in the corpus: same setup, different trigger timeframe,
different outcome.** And it is stated in advance of our looking.

## THE NEWS-BEHAVIOUR RULES — `[stated]`
> *"what NFP usually does is that it makes a move and it makes that move until market opens and
> then we usually reverse that move. CPI is the opposite. CPI usually makes a move and it keeps
> making that move for the entire day."* `[@ 03:58]` `[trader-claimed, unverified]`

**Two distinct, mechanical, event-conditional claims.** We hold the news calendar, so both are
testable.

## THE NO-BOTTOM-WICK GAP — `[stated]`
> *"It is a 15-minute gap with no bottom wick. These are super sus. We want to see these get
> taken. Having a candle with no bottom wick is extremely rare that it just leaves it and never
> comes back. This goes mostly for 4-hour candles."* `[@ 05:37]`

## RISK BAND — restated
> *"don't take anything lower than one to three. 1 to 3 and up is pretty good for prop firms.
> 1 to 3 to 1 to 6 is the sweet spot for me. Rarely take 1 to 3s. My personal lowest is like
> 1 to 4."* `[@ 15:56]`

## The 25%-of-rejection-block variant
> *"you could have used the 25% of the rejection block which does end up playing out for a 5.6,
> or the CE which is also really good but it would have gotten edged and you would not have
> gotten in the trade."* `[@ 10:49]`

**A third entry point on the same object: start of wick / 25% / CE.** Each is a different
fill-rate-vs-risk trade-off and all three are stated by him.

## The hybrid
> *"You can even take one minute entry triggers out of five minutes. Just wait for five minute
> confirmation and then I'm going to look for a one minute entry. It's like a little hybrid."*
> `[@ 16:44]`
