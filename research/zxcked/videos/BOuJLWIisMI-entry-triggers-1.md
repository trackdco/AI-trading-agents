## THE TRIGGERS — 1 to 4, all `[stated]`

**1. Straight at the level (limit).**
> *"pretty much only is valid if you have this engineered liquidity… you can enter straight at
> the level with a 10-point stop, five point stop, whatever you want, depending on the level."*
> `[@ 01:30]`
**The engineered-liquidity gate is what licenses a blind limit.** Without it, he wants a trigger.

**2. Rejection block.**
> *"it's basically the wick that rejects the level… you're going to just use the wick that it
> makes. It doesn't have to be the 50% mark."* `[@ 01:52]`

**3. Change in state of delivery (CISD)**, on the 1-minute `[@ 02:42]`.
> *"you're entering on this confirmation and you're putting your stop above this level or that
> high that it made after tapping the level."* `[@ 03:05]`

**4. Inverse fair value gap** `[@ 04:16]` — continues in #2.

## The stop floor — `[stated]`
> *"in this case it would have been a 3.5 point stop, which is just kind of ridiculous. I would
> probably put it at five points… for me five points is just minimum. I used to do crazy trades
> like two point stops, three point stops… I find like five points is more than enough."*
> `[@ 03:28]`

**A stated 5-point minimum stop.** This is a hard number and it is directly implementable.
