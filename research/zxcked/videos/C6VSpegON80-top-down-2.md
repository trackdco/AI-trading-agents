## THE SMT INVERSION — worth isolating
Step 3 states that **SMT at a liquidity pool INVALIDATES that pool as a target**, because the
divergence means it is less likely to be taken. In Wick Theory #4 `[W_2x8Mv0-9M @ 03:17]`, SMT
**at the wick you are entering from** is an *enhancer*.

**These are consistent, not contradictory**: SMT against the level you want taken is bad; SMT at
the level you are fading is good. Recorded explicitly so nobody later reads it as a conflict.
